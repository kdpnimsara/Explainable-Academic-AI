# SLIIT Public CourseWeb Calendar – Access and Snapshot Limitations

**Checked:** 14 November 2026
**Source authority:** SLIIT CourseWeb
**Source URL:** https://courseweb.sliit.lk/course/index.php?categoryid=2117

## What is publicly visible

The public CourseWeb page exposes a Calendar block and semester category links. The public view checked on 14 November 2026 displayed November 2026 and month navigation to October and December. The calendar view showed event counts on selected days, but the public page did not provide a complete official academic calendar document or a full set of event details in the accessible snapshot. Course content and student-specific notices require SLIIT login.

## How this source should be used

This is an **archive/access note only**, not an academic calendar and not an examination timetable. It is deliberately marked `index: false` in the SLIIT manifest so that the limited public calendar snapshot is not used as answer evidence.

For exact semester dates, examination dates, registration deadlines, holidays, or timetable changes, students must consult the latest official CourseWeb notices and the relevant faculty/Academic Affairs communications. Do not infer dates from this snapshot.
