SLIIT Business School grants a full scholarship to Amandi Madanayake
Amandi Madanayake, 20 years old, had always been confident of achieving success. Her goal in life was to become a professional lecturer, bringing new concepts to the field of Business, helping professionals to soar. Her journey to achieve her ambitions was well thought out. A past student of Sanghamitta Balika Vidyalaya, Galle, she obtained 9 As at the Ordinary Level Examination and emerged first all-island in the Commerce stream at the GCE Advanced Level Examination in 2020, having excelled in Accounting, Business Studies and Information Technology.

Island Rankers Choose SLIIT for Their Higher Education
SLIIT is proud to welcome some of Sri Lanka’s top-performing students who achieved exceptional island rankings in the GCE A/L examinations. These talented individuals, ranked 1st, 2nd, and 3rd in streams such as Engineering Technology, Bio Systems Technology, Arts, and Commerce, have chosen SLIIT as their university destination.
Their decision to pursue higher education with us reflects the trust and recognition SLIIT has earned as a leading institution for academic excellence and innovation. We are honored to support their journey with full scholarships and provide an environment that fosters their continued success.

Nena Diriya Future Leaders Scholarship
Offered through the Nena Diriya Endowment Fund, this scholarship grants a full tuition waiver and monthly stipend to students with the highest national G.C.E. A/L results in their respective discipline, fostering academic excellence.
Scholarships for Outstanding Performance in A/L Examination
Awarded to students who achieve 3 As in local GCE A/L or 3 A* s /4A* s in one sitting of Cambridge/Edexcel A/L exams. Th ese merit-based scholarship s are offered annually, subject to availability and specific terms and conditions.
Scholarships for Outstanding Performance in School-Based Sports & Extra-Curricular Activities
This scholarship recognizes students who have excelled in sports or extra-curricular activities at international, national, or provincial levels during school. Awards are granted based on SLIIT’s evaluation criteria for non-academic excellence.
Scholarships for Sports and Extra-Curricular Activities
SLIIT rewards top academic performers each semester with full or partial tuition fee waivers. Students ranking within the top 1%–2% in their respective programs are considered.
Sports and Extra-Curricular Scholarships
SLIIT offers scholarships to students who excel in sports and extra-curricular activities during their academic tenure. Achievements at the provincial, national, or international levels are considered.
Need-Based & Charity Organization Scholarships
SLIIT offers additional scholarships funded by charitable organizations and based on financial need. These are awarded to eligible continuing students who demonstrate hardship and academic potential.
