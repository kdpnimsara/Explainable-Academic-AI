Am I a Domestic or an International student?
Your student status depends on your nationality and residency. Domestic students are Sri Lankan citizens or residents, while international students hold foreign passports or reside outside Sri Lanka. Understanding your status helps with admission and fee details.
Domestic students
International students
Domestic students are citizens or permanent residents of Sri Lanka. This includes those who have completed their schooling under a local curriculum such as the GCE A/L or London curriculum or equivalent. As a domestic student, you will follow standard local fee structures and admission processes.
International students are applicants who are not citizens or permanent residents of Sri Lanka. This includes students holding foreign passports, residing overseas, or having completed international curricula. Specific admission guidelines, visa support, and fee structures apply.

Payments to SLIIT
The fees should be paid to Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT) through any branch of Bank of Ceylon, Sampath Bank, or Hatton National Bank.
Fees paid are normally non-refundable. However, refund requests may be considered subject to the terms and conditions signed at the point of enrolment.

FAQs
Are credit card payments accepted?
Yes, SLIIT accepts credit card payments via the SLIIT Online Payment Portal. Students can conveniently use Visa or Mastercard to settle their program fees online at pay.sliit.lk
What are the current program fees?
Program fees vary by degree and academic year. Please refer to the above fee structure or contact our call center agents for the most up-to-date program fees.
What scholarships are available at SLIIT?
SLIIT offers various scholarships. These include Nena Diriya , Virusara Privilege, and semester-based academic performance waivers , etc. You may visit the scholarship page for more information.
