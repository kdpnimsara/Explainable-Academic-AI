Bank Loan Support
SLIIT offers dedicated bank loan assistance to help students finance their education with ease. Through partnerships with leading banks, we provide streamlined loan application guidance, flexible repayment plans, and personalized support. Our goal is to ensure that financial concerns don’t stand in the way of your academic success at SLIIT.
Bellow details are subject to change depending on bank decisions
For further details please contact your nearest Bank of Ceylon Branch Thilini (Credit Department) –071 660 2855
Or Log on to https://www.boc.lk/

Maximum period of the loan - course duration + 4 years (maximum)

up to 5 years - monthly AWPLR + 3.0% p.a. (floor rate - 22%)

FAQs
What are the bank loan facilities available for SLIIT students?
SLIIT students can apply for educational loans through selected partner banks offering customized loan schemes to support tuition fee payments.
Can I apply for a loan before receiving my SLIIT admission confirmation?
Most banks require a valid offer or confirmation letter from SLIIT. It’s best to begin your application once you receive official documentation.
Are there any interest-free loan options?
Yes. The Sri Lankan Ministry of Education offers an Interest-Free Student Loan Scheme for eligible students enrolling in selected degree programmes at institutions like SLIIT.
To learn more and check your eligibility, visit the official page here: https://www.sliit.lk/study/fees-and-scholarships/government-loan-schemes
How long does it take to process an education loan?
Loan processing typically takes between 5 to 10 working days, depending on the bank and documentation provided.
Whom can I contact SLIIT for more information?
Please reach out to the SLIIT Finance Division at +94 11 754 4801 or email info@sliit.lk for guidance on loan letters, documentation, and partner bank information.
