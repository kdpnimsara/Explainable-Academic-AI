# SLIIT Student Rules and Regulations – Public Overview

**Source publication date:** 24 January 2021
**Source authority:** SLIIT Academic Affairs (Registry) / SLIIT Support Desk
**Source URL:** https://support.sliit.lk/kb/articles/pdf/rules-and-regulations-for-students

## Student Rule Book

SLIIT's public Support Desk article explains that the Student Disciplinary Rules and Academic Administrative Regulations are commonly referred to as the Student Rule Book. The article identifies the **10th Edition – 2018** and states that it is available through CourseWeb at: Support Services → Student Affairs → Institute Manuals → Student Rule Book.

## Rules that may apply to students

The article states that SLIIT students are bound by applicable Student Disciplinary Rules and Academic Administrative Regulations, including admission and registration regulations, as well as applicable rules for library, laboratories, hostel use (where relevant), IT policy, and other SLIIT regulations.

## SLIIT programmes and partner-university programmes

The article distinguishes examination regulations as follows:

- Students following diplomas and degrees offered by SLIIT are subject to SLIIT Examination Regulations.
- Students following programmes leading to degrees offered by SLIIT partner universities are subject to the examination regulations of the applicable partner university.

## Important version and access limitations

- The public article is dated 24 January 2021 and refers to the Student Rule Book 10th Edition – 2018. It does not reproduce the complete Rule Book.
- Do not assume this is the latest edition or that every provision remains unchanged. Students should consult the current Rule Book and notices through their official CourseWeb access.
- Do not apply SLIIT examination rules to a partner-university award without first identifying the awarding university and applicable regulations.
