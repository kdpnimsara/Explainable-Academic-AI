Conferences
Highlighting SLIIT-hosted conferences fostering knowledge exchange, innovation, and global research collaborations
Research
Conferences
SLIIT actively hosts and participates in national and international conferences, providing platforms for knowledge exchange and networking. Our researchers regularly present at prestigious forums worldwide, and the institution supports faculty attendance through dedicated funding mechanisms.
SLIIT also organizes flagship conferences under each Faculty that bring together academics, industry leaders, and policymakers to address pressing challenges in technology, engineering, sustainability, and business. These events showcase the institution’s leadership in convening research communities and fostering collaborative solutions
ICAC - International Conference on Advancements in Computing
An international platform hosted by SLIIT’s Faculty of Computing, ICAC brings together researchers and industry experts to share innovations, present applied research, and explore emerging technologies that shape the future of computing and contribute to global well-being.
Read More
ICSDB - International Conference on Sustainable & Digital Business
Organized by SLIIT Business School, ICSDB is a global platform for academics and professionals to share innovative research and real-world insights on sustainability and digital transformation in business, fostering collaboration and impactful solutions .
Read More
SICASH - SLIIT International Conference on Advancements in Sciences and Humanities
SICASH, the flagship annual multi-conference of the Faculty of Humanities and Sciences at SLIIT, provides a platform for local and international scholars to share research across diverse disciplines, driving academic dialogue and societal impact .
Read More
SICET - SLIIT International Conference on Engineering and Technology
Hosted by the Faculty of Engineering at SLIIT, SICET is a dynamic platform for academics, students, and industry professionals to present research in Engineering, Technology, and Sciences, encouraging innovation and multidisciplinary collaboration across theory, practice, and education .
Read More
More about Conferences
Publishing Opportunities for Presenters
SLIIT provides presenters with opportunities to publish their research in conference proceedings and affiliated journals. Selected high-quality papers may be considered for fast-track submission in Scopus-indexed or partner publications. Much like the model used by many global Universities, this approach ensures your research receives broad academic visibility and citation impact, fostering further academic advancement.
Global Networking and Collaboration
SLIIT conferences are designed to encourage global academic and industry interaction. Participants can build professional networks, initiate research collaborations, and explore post-conference partnerships. Inspired by events hosted by the global universities, our conferences connect local talent with global expertise, paving the way for cross-border innovation and joint publications.
Workshops, Panels, and Keynotes
In addition to research presentations, SLIIT hosts expert-led workshops, keynote sessions, and thematic panel discussions on emerging topics. These sessions provide practical insights and promote dialogue across disciplines. They equip participants with updated knowledge and skills relevant to academia, industry, and research commercialization.
Support for Early-Career Researchers
SLIIT encourages participation from early-career researchers and postgraduate students by offering special sessions, mentoring opportunities, and awards for best papers. Our conferences foster skill development, confidence building, and early exposure to academic publishing, helping the next generation of researchers grow professionally and contribute meaningfully.
