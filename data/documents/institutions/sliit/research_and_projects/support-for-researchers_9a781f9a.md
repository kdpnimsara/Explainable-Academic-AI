Support for Researchers
SLIIT provides comprehensive support to empower researchers at every stage of their careers, offering funding, training, resources, and expert guidance to achieve high-quality, impactful outcomes.
Research
Support For Researchers
Comprehensive Research Support at SLIIT
SLIIT provides extensive support for researchers, including internal research grants, external funding assistance, ethics review processes, and research training workshops to enhance research output and quality. Our resources are tailored to foster innovation, collaboration, and impactful research across disciplines.
View Available Grants
Internal Research Grants
SLIIT offers competitive internal research grants to seed innovation and support both early-career and experienced researchers in building impactful research portfolios.
Read More
External Research Grants
We assist researchers in securing external grants, connecting with national and international funding bodies to expand the scale and scope of research.
Read More
Library & Online Resources
SLIIT researchers have access to extensive physical and digital resources, including academic journals, research databases, industry standards, e-books, and reference materials. These comprehensive collections support advanced research, literature reviews, and academic publishing across all faculties and disciplines
SLIIT Library
Research Centers, Labs & Equipment
Each SL IIT faculty houses state-of-the-art laboratories tailored to their discipline—including Computing, Engineering, Sciences, Business, Architecture, and Law. These facilities are regularly upgraded with the latest equipment, software, and analytical tools, fostering innovation and hands-on research experiences that support both academic inquiry and industry collaboration.
Research Facilities
How can I apply for a SLIIT Research Grant?
Complete and submit the required proposal documents to the PVC (R&I) Office by the specified deadline. Your proposal will be reviewed by independent review ers befo re Senate and MCC approval. This is applicat to SLIIT Academics only.
What are the funding limits for research grants?
SLIIT Academic can currently receive up to LKR 500,000 per year. For MPhil/PhD research students, this can increase to LKR 800,000 with certain conditions.
Can I get reimbursement for journal publication fees?
Yes. Reimbursement is available based on the indexing of the journal (Scopus Top 10%, Q1, Q2, etc.) and subject to approval.
Is there funding for conference participation?
Yes. Travel, registration, and accommodation expenses can be partially covered for presenting accepted full papers at eligible national and international conferences.
Who do I contact for research ethics or IRB-related concerns?
All research-related compliance is overseen by the PVC (R&I) Office in the New Academic building at SLIIT Malabe Campus
Key Initiatives Supporting Researchers
Annual Internal Research Grants:
Competitive funding for faculty and postgraduate researchers
Performance-Based Monthly Research Allowance:
Recognition and reward for high-quality scholarly output
International Conference Support:
Financial assistance for presenting research globally
Journal Publication Support:
Financial assistance for to cover Article Processing Charges (APCs) for faculty and postgraduate researchers publishing in Scopus-indexed journals.
Research Ethics and IP Advisory:
Dedicated frameworks for responsible research conduct and intellectual property protection
Industry Collaboration Support:
Pathways for commercialization, consultancy, and applied research
Access to Digital Resources:
Comprehensive subscriptions to major academic databases and research tools
