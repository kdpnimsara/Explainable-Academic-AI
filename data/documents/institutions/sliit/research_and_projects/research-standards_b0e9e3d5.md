Research Standards
Ensuring ethical, high-quality research with integrity, innovation, and global standards to advance knowledge and academic excellence.

Research Standards
At SLIIT, research is not just a pursuit—it defines who we are. Our commitment to innovation and intellectual curiosity is embodied by the SLIIT Research & International Centre, a dynamic hub where academic staff and students collaborate to explore new ideas and develop creative solutions.
The centre is supported by senior academics and researchers, fostering interdisciplinary research and impactful discoveries.

Research Grants and Funding
SLIIT has secured over Rs. 125 million in research grants from the government, along with Rs. 62.5 million in grants from the AHEAD World Bank project, which includes six funded projects, one national patent, one U.S. patent, and 30 indexed journal publications. The Research Grant Scheme further enhances funding opportunities, supporting pioneering research initiatives.
Global Recognition and Visibility
We actively showcase our research on international platforms, ensuring that SLIIT’s research output gains global visibility and recognition.
PhD Strength
SLIIT is home to over 95 PhD holders, contributing to academic excellence and innovation. Their expertise fosters a culture of research and knowledge across diverse disciplines.
Top 2% Scientists
Six SLIIT academics are ranked among the top 2% of researchers globally in the prestigious Stanford-Elsevier rankings. This recognition highlights their outstanding contributions to scientific research and innovation.
Scopus Publications
In the past five years, SLIIT has produced 1,190 Scopus-indexed publications, demonstrating a strong commitment to impactful research and advancing knowledge across various fields.
Research Centers of Excellence
SLIIT hosts Centers of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence & Informatics and Electronics & Transmission (CIET), driving research, industry collaborations, and technological advancements in these cutting-edge fields.
Innovation and Collaboration
Through the Sri Lanka Technology Incubator, SLIIT supports startups, transforming successful research projects into scalable solutions. Researchers worldwide collaborate and share insights at renowned academic conferences.
Advanced Research Degrees
Offers M.Phil. and Ph.D. programs in key disciplines.
Expanding to include Humanities and Sciences.
Provides expert mentorship for postgraduate researchers.
Research Clusters and Grants
Each department at SLIIT has dedicated research clusters led by senior academics, supported by annual grants and mentorship programs that nurture fresh ideas and innovation.
Comprehensive Research Education
Postgraduate programs at SLIIT integrate strong research methodologies, ensuring that students develop essential research skills under expert faculty guidance.
Resources and Support
Our research grants provide access to online publications, conference participation, and other essential scholarly resources, ensuring that students and researchers have the tools they need to succeed.
A Supportive Research Environment
The Pro Vice-Chancellor (Research and International) office at Malabe Campus plays a key role in ensuring a nurturing and well-supported research ecosystem.
Through these initiatives, SLIIT continues to drive knowledge advancement, foster innovation, and solidify its standing as a leading research institution in Sri Lanka and beyond.
