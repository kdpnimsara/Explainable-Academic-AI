SICET - SLIIT International Conference on Engineering and Technology
Hosted by the Faculty of Engineering at SLIIT, SICET is a dynamic platform for academics, students, and industry professionals to present research in Engineering, Technology, and Sciences, encouraging innovation and multidisciplinary collaboration across theory, practice, and education .
The SLIIT International Conference on Engineering and Technology (SICET) welcomes research contributions spanning emerging trends in Engineering, Technology, and Applied and Natural Sciences. Conducted in a hybrid format, the conference fosters meaningful exchanges between local and international participants, providing opportunities for knowledge sharing, collaborative exploration, and the advancement of solutions with real-world impact .
