SLIIT Journals Publications
Showcasing SLIIT’s scholarly journals featuring original research, insights, and advancements across diverse disciplines.
Research
Journals
SLIIT encourages and supports the publication of high-quality research through its own academic journals and contributions to international peer-reviewed journals. These publications cover a wide range of disciplines including computing, engineering, business, and the humanities.
Faculty and students are guided to publish in reputable indexed platforms, ensuring global visibility and scholarly impact. Editorial boards consist of experienced researchers, and all publications follow strict ethical and academic standards to ensure credibility, transparency, and rigor in every issue.

More about Journals
SLIIT-Hosted Academic Journals
SLIIT hosts several academic journals that provide a platform for local and international scholars to share original research. These journals are managed by editorial boards with subject matter experts and undergo peer-review processes. Aligned with global best practices, these journals help elevate research standards and broaden visibility across the academic community.
Encouraging Student Publications
SLIIT motivates undergraduate and postgraduate students to publish their research in institutional or external journals. Student research is often featured in special issues or student-focused editions, with faculty mentorship guiding them through the publishing process. This aligns with practices at global universities where early exposure to academic publishing enhances learning and career development.
International Indexing and Visibility
SLIIT journals are in the process of aligning with global indexing requirements (e.g. , Scopus ). Faculty are also encouraged to publish in top-tier indexed journals, increasing the reach and citation impact of their research. SLIIT emphasizes research that meets global publication standards and contributes to the international academic community.
Editorial and Peer Review Excellence
Editorial boards at SLIIT follow transparent peer-review processes, ensuring the quality, relevance, and originality of published articles. Editors and reviewers are selected for their expertise and international experience. Inspired by global editorial frameworks, SLIIT upholds rigorous academic integrity and supports continuous improvement in its publication process.
