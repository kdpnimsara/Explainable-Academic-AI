SICASH - SLIIT International Conference on Advancements in Sciences and Humanities
SICASH, the flagship annual multi-conference of the Faculty of Humanities and Sciences at SLIIT, provides a platform for local and international scholars to share research across diverse disciplines, driving academic dialogue and societal impact .
The SLIIT International Conference on Advancements in Sciences and Humanities (SICASH) returns for its sixth edition under the theme “Transforming Possibilities Through Research Excellence.” Organized by the Faculty of Humanities and Sciences, SICASH welcomes research submissions in Applied Sciences, Education, Law, English Literature, Nursing, Psychology, Mathematics, and beyond, fostering multidisciplinary knowledge exchange and scholarly collaboration .
