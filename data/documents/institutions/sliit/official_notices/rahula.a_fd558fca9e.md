Academic Staff
Rahula.a
CAREER SUMMARY
Deputy Vice-Chancellor, University of Moratuwa, 2012- 2017
Senior Professor in Mechanical Engineering, Dept. of Mechanical Engineering, University of Moratuwa, 2011 to 2019
Professor in Mechanical Engineering, Dept. of Mechanical Engineering, University of Moratuwa, 2003 to 2011
Head Dept of Mechanical Engineering, University of Moratuwa, 2002-2005
Senior Lecturer in Mechanical Engineering, Dept. of Mechanical Engineering, University of Moratuwa, 1993 to 2003
Director, Local Technical Secretariat (LTS) of the University of Moratuwa for the World Bank IRQUE project, under the Ministry of Higher Education, 2006-2010
Director, Postgraduate Studies Division, Faculty of Engineering, University of Moratuwa, 2008-2012
Director, Operational Technical Secretariat (OTS) of the University of Moratuwa for the World Bank HETC project, under the Ministry of Higher Education, 2011-2012
Chairman National Institute of Fisheries & Nautical Engineering, Sri Lanka, 2004-2005
Director, Board of Directors Sri Lanka Transports Board, 2006
Commissioner, Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka, 2009 to 2019
Visiting Professor, Toyohashi University of Technology, Japan, 2006-2008
Visiting Faculty Energy Program Asian Institute of Technology, Thailand, 1998-1999
ACADEMIC QUALIFICATIONS
PhD in Energy Engineering, Ecole Nationale Superieure des Mines de Paris, France, 1992
Degree of Profound Studies (D.E.A), Energy Engineering, Ecole Nationale Superieure des Mines de Paris, 1988
Degree of Master of Engineering in Energy Technology, Asian Institute of Technology, Thailand, 1986
Bachelor of the Science of Engineering (Hons), Mechanical Engineering Specialization, Univ of Moratuwa, 1982
HONOURS AND AWARDS

French Government Scholarship for Doctoral studies in France, 1986
Outstanding Research Award, University of Moratuwa 1999, 2000, 2001, 2002, 2003
Presidential Awards for Scientific Publications for the years 2014, 2015 & 2017
Honorary Distinction of "Order of the Academic Palms" award by the Government of France 2018

RESEARCH CLUSTERS
Energy and Sustainability
Interdisciplinary research on sustainable energy solutions, policy frameworks, and green technologies to combat climate change.
Thermal Engineering
The thermal engineering research unit deals with the effective storage and use of thermal energy. Research also looks at the use of renewable energy for local engineering applications. Studies include heat and mass transfers based on simulations implement innovative heating and cooling technologies and their application in engineering processes involving heat transfer.
ONGOING RESEARCH
Solar pond based thermal energy utilization for desalination of water
Performance-based approach for evaluating naturally ventilated buildings
Optimization methodologies for Building Performance
Multi-objective Optimization in designing standalone hybrid energy systems
Microalgae based biofuels and value-added products
