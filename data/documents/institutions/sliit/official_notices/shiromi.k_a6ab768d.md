Prof. Shiromi Karunaratne
Professor | Department of Civil Engineering | Faculty of Engineering
Academic Staff
Shiromi.k
CAREER SUMMARY
I am a Professor in Civil Engineering in the Faculty of Engineering, Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology, Malabe, Sri Lanka.
09/2020-todate- Professor in Civil Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology, Sri Lanka.
05/2015 to 09/2020 – Senior Lecturer in Civil Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology, Sri Lanka.
10/2013 to 09/2014 – Head of the Department of Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology, Sri Lanka
06/2004 to 04/2015 – Senior Lecturer, Department of Earth Resources Engineering, University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka.
04/2010 to 03/ 2013 – Head of the Department, Department of Earth Resources Engineering, University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka
09/2001 to 09/ 2003 – Post Doctoral Research Fellow, Saitama University, Japan.
1996 to 2001 – Research Assistant, Saitama University, Japan.
01/1995 to 01/1996 – Civil Engineer, The Consortium of Itochu- Corporation and Sri U-Thong co. Ltd, Sri Lanka
08/1994 to 01/1995 – Civil Engineer, Environmental Engineering Consultants, Sri Lanka
03/1994 to 08/1994 – Instructor, University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka.
ACADEMIC QUALIFICATIONS
BSc Eng. (Hons) in Civil Engineering, University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka, 1994
M.Eng. in Environmental Science and Human Engineering, Saitama University, Japan, 1998
Ph.D in Environmental Science and Human Engineering, Saitama University, Japan, 2001
HONOURS AND AWARDS
1996 Monbukugakusho Scholarship – Awarded by the Japanese Government for a Master Degree
1998 Monbukugakusho Scholarship – Awarded by the Japanese Government for a Doctoral Degree
2001 Postdoctoral Fellowship for Foreign Researcher – Awarded by the Japan Society for the Promotion of Science
RESEARCH INTERESTS AND MEMBERSHIP
Research Interests
My research interests are sustainable consumption and production in engineering (life cycle approach, sustainable construction material/recycling/reuse of material, cleaner production etc) water and waste water treatment and sanitation (low cost wastewater treatment technologies, sustainable water usage and irrigation water efficiency, climate impact issues and sustainable management/utilisation of resources etc). Solid/construction waste reuse and management etc.
Memberships
Charted Member of Institute of Engineers Sri Lanka (IESL)
Charted Member of Institute of Practising Environmental Professionals Sri Lanka (IEPSL)
Member of the Sri Lanka Association for Advancement of Science
RESEARCH CLUSTERS
Sustainability in Built Environments
"The built environment touches all aspects of our lives, encompassing the buildings we live in, the distribution systems that provide us with water and electricity, and the roads, bridges, and transportation systems we use to get from place to place. Our research group focuses on research areas such as whole building life cycle assessment, Life cycle costing and Life cycle data for construction materials, Green buildings and sustainable building material research, sustainable management of wastewater and solid waste, drinking water treatment, air pollution and control etc. Areas: Building Life cycle assessment, circular economy in construction, Sustainability assessment of buildings, building materials, machinery, and transportation Wastewater management, Drinking water treatment, solid waste management, air and noise pollution, water sensitive urban design, water pollution mitigation and management"
Energy and Sustainability
Interdisciplinary research on sustainable energy solutions, policy frameworks, and green technologies to combat climate change.
ONGOING RESEARCH
Life cycle assessment of buildings- a modelling approach
Climate Change Impacts on Urban Water Quality
