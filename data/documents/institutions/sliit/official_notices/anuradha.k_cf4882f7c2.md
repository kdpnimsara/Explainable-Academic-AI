Prof. Anuradha Karunasena
Associate Dean (Academic) | Department of Information Technology | Faculty of Computing
Academic Staff
Anuradha.k
CAREER SUMMARY
Anuradha Karunasena joined Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology as a temporary Instructor in Information Technology in year 2005 and served in the positions of Assistant Lecturer and Lecturer. Currently she is a senior Lecturer (Higher Grade) attached to the Department of Information Technology. She also chairs the Quality Cell of the Faculty of Computing.
Senior Lecturer (Higher Grade) - September 2015 - to date
Head/Staff Development Center - December 2016 - to date
Chair person - Faculty Quality Cell(Faculty of Computing) - January 2016 - to date Program
Coordinator - IT program - January 2018 - to date
ACADEMIC QUALIFICATIONS
Ph.D. in Business Information Systems, RMIT University, Australia, 2015
M.Sc. in Information Technology, Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology, Sri Lanka, 2009
B.Sc. Special (Hons) Degree in Information Technology, Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology, Sri Lanka, 2006
B.Sc. in Information Technology, Curtin University, Australia, 2005
SEDA Accredited Senior Teacher in Higher Education
HONOURS AND AWARDS
Nominee for Australian Council of Professors and Heads of Information Systems (ACPHIS) Best Australian Information Systems PhD Thesis Award, 2016

Memberships

RESEARCH CLUSTERS

A multidisciplinary initiative advancing AI research in machine learning, NLP, and big data analytics to solve complex global challenges and drive intelligent decision-making.
