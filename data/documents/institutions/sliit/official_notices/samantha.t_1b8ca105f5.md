CAREER SUMMARY
Prof. Samantha Thelijjagoda is a Senior Professor in the Department of Computer Systems Engineering, Faculty of Computing, at the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT). With over 25 years of experience in higher education, academic leadership, research, and international collaboration, he has held several senior leadership positions at SLIIT, including Pro Vice-Chancellor (Research & International), Dean of SLIIT Business School, Dean of the Faculty of Graduate Studies and Research, and Head of academic departments. Currently he is on his sabbatical leave.
He holds a PhD and MEng from Gifu University, Japan, and a First-Class Honours degree in Statistics from the University of Sri Jayewardenepura, Sri Lanka. He is a Senior Member of IEEE, a member of IET and CSSL, and the country representative for IFIP Technical Committee 8 (Information Systems).
His research interests cover a broad range of emerging technologies, particularly Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, Human-Computer Interaction, predictive analytics, intelligent decision-support systems, and AI-driven applications in education and healthcare. He is particularly interested in the responsible application of AI to address real-world challenges, including elderly care, assistive learning, digital health, educational forecasting, and human-AI interaction. His research approach emphasizes the development of practical, explainable, and validated technological solutions with meaningful societal impact.
His academic and administrative appointments at SLIIT include:

2023.01 to 2025.05 – Pro Vice-Chancellor (Research & International)
2018.01 to 2022.12 – Dean, SLIIT Business School

2016.12 to 2017.03 – Acting Dean, Faculty of Graduate Studies and Research
2016.12 to 2025.03 – Professor in Information Systems Engineering, Faculty of Computing
2015.01 to 2016.11 – Head, Department of Information Systems Engineering, Faculty of Computing
2012.01 to 2014.12 – Head, Department of Information Management, Faculty of Business
2009.09 to 2015.12 – Postgraduate Course Coordinator, Faculty of Graduate Studies and Research
2008.09 to 2016.11 – Senior Lecturer (Higher Grade)
2007.09 to 2008.08 – Senior Lecturer
