Academic Staff

CAREER SUMMARY
Designations
Professor, Department of ISE, Faculty of Computing - SLIIT
Associate Dean (Research) - Faculty of Computing
ACADEMIC QUALIFICATIONS
PhD (IS) - Curtin University, Australia (2017)
MSc. (IT) - Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (2006)
BSc. Sp. Hons. (IT) – Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (2004)
BSc. (IT) - Curtin University, Australia (2003)
HONOURS AND AWARDS
NBQSA 2021 - Silver Award (Tertiary Projects) (LandVal-Data-driven Online Decision Support for Hotel Site Selection - Main Supervisor)
APICTA 2019 - Merit award at the 19th APICTA Awards 2019 ( CropMedic+ -Intelligent Plant Diseases Identification, and Dispersion Forecasting System - Co-supervisor)
NBQSA 2019 - Bronze Award for Tertiary Projects, Technology Category ( CropMedic+ -Intelligent Plant Diseases Identification, and Dispersion Forecasting System - Co-supervisor)
From over 400 submissions, CropMedic+ was shortlisted among the 22 projects in the winning circle for WSA (World Summit Awards) Young Innovators 2019 (a special recognition for young social entrepreneurs under 26 years of age, using ICTs to take action on the UN Sustainable Development Goals).
Scholarly & Professional Awards:
Curtin Alumni Professional Excellence Award 2025
Curtin Offshore Partner Research Scholarship – Curtin University 2010.
SLIIT scholarship for superior academic performance in undergraduate studies 2003.
Award for 15 Years of Service to SLIIT.
Award for 10 Years of Service to SLIIT.
RESEARCH INTERESTS AND MEMBERSHIP
Research Interests
Technology Enhanced Education
Social Network Analysis
Requirements Engineering
Business Process Management
Social Capital
Memberships
Senior Member of Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers - SMIEEE
Member of Institution of Engineering and Technology -MIET
Member of Computer Society of Sri Lanka - MCSSL
Member of Association of Information Systems - MAIS
Member of Special Interest Group in ICT for Global Development - SigGlobDev
RESEARCH CLUSTERS
Technology Integration & Management (TIM)
Bridging technology and business through research on human-computer interaction, ethical tech integration, and strategic frameworks for digital transformation.
Software Systems & Technologies (SST)
Driving innovation in software engineering, high-performance computing, and scalable architectures to build robust, next-generation applications and systems.
