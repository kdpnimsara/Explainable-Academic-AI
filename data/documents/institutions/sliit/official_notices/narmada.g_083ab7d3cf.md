Ms. Narmada Gamage
Lecturer | Department of Computer Systems Engineering | Faculty of Computing
Academic Staff
Narmada.g
CAREER SUMMARY
Narmada Gamage is a Lecturer attached to the Department of Computer Systems Engineering, Faculty of Computing, Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology.She completed her bachelor’s degree in ‘Information Technology’ with a First Class Honours at Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology and entered the academia by joining Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology as an Academic Instructor. She completed her master's degree in Business Analytics from University of Colombo School of Computing
Furthermore, she is an active member of SLIIT Toastmasters club and faculty of computing timetabling team. Her research interests are Assistive Technology, Business Analytics and Machine Learning.
April 2022 - Present : Lecturer , Department of Computer Systems Engineering, Faculty of Computing, Sri Lanka Institute of Information
April 2018 – 2022 : Assistant Lecturer , Department of Computer Systems Engineering, Faculty of Computing, Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology, Sri Lanka
July 2017 – April 2018 : Instructor, Department of Computer Systems Engineering, Faculty of Computing, Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology, Sri Lanka
September 2016 – July 2017 : Research Assistant, Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology, Sri Lanka
