WHAT ARE THE PROGRAM OUTCOMES
The SLIIT’s B.Ed. (Hons) in Physical Sciences offers specialized training in teaching Physics and Mathematics, equipping students with both subject mastery and effective pedagogical skills. The curriculum is carefully aligned with national education standards, ensuring graduates are prepared to meet the expectations of today’s educational systems.
Students gain valuable hands-on experience through teaching practice in real classroom settings, allowing them to develop confidence and competence as educators. The program also provides access to modern laboratories and a range of advanced teaching resources, fostering an engaging and practical learning environment.
Graduates are well-prepared for rewarding teaching careers in both public and private educational institutions, locally and internationally.
This program covers both theoretical and practical aspect into ,
Educational Psychology and Pedagogy
Teaching Methodologies for Physics and Mathematics
Advanced Physics and Mathematics Modules
Curriculum Development and Assessment Strategies
Research Methods in Education
Upon successful completion of the program at SLIIT, graduate will ,
Demonstrate proficiency in Physics and Mathematics subject matter.
Apply effective teaching strategies in secondary education.
Design and implement curriculum plans aligned with educational standards.
Utilize assessment tools to evaluate student learning outcomes.
Engage in continuous professional development in the field of education.

Course Summary
Local A/Ls: Minimum of 3 “S” passes including Combined Mathematics & Physics in one and the same sitting.
Cambridge/Edexcel A/Ls: Minimum of 3 “D” passes including Mathematics & Physics in one and the same sitting.
Applicants should also pass the Aptitude Test conducted by SLIIT.
-
The fee for this degree is LKR 240,000 per semester. Fees for each semester must be paid before commencement of the semester.
The course fee is all-inclusive, covering lectures, tutorials, examinations, and access to library and computer laboratory facilities.
Course fees are subject to change.
Fees can be paid to the bank accounts of SLIIT at any branch. For further details on payment options and bank information, please visit:
https://www.sliit.lk/study/fees-and-scholarships/fees-and-costs?#payment-to-sliit
The B.Ed. (Hons) in Physical Sciences at SLIIT is a four-year full-time degree program conducted in English. It is approved by the Ministry of Education, Sri Lanka, and recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC). The program includes structured teaching practice, allowing students to gain hands-on experience in real classroom environments.
In the final year, students are required to complete a research dissertation, promoting critical thinking and academic inquiry. The program comprises a minimum of 120 credits and is aligned with SLQF Level 6 standards.
FAQs
Is the BEd (Hons) in Physical Sciences degree recognized?
Yes, it is approved by the Ministry of Education and recognized by the UGC.
What career opportunities are available after completing this degree?
Graduates can become secondary school teachers, curriculum developers, or pursue postgraduate studies in education or science.
Does this programme include practical teaching experience?
Yes, students engage in teaching practice sessions in educational settings.
