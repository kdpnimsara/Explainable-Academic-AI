Step Into the World of Computing
Welcome to the Faculty of Computing at SLIIT – your gateway to future-ready technology education. We offer a broad range of programs that cover the full spectrum of computing, from Information Technology and Software Engineering to Computer Science and Computer Systems Engineering. With expert lecturers, advanced computer labs, and a strong focus on practical and project-based learning, we equip students with the skills needed to thrive in a competitive global landscape.
Our degrees are UGC-approved and recognised internationally. Students can also access partner university pathways in the UK, USA, Australia, and Canada, or opt for Curtin University offshore programs offered at SLIIT. As a member of the Association of Commonwealth Universities and IAU, we are committed to academic excellence, innovation, and industry relevance in computing education.

Explore Our Facilities
Computer Labs (PC Labs)
The Computer Labs provides a dedicated space for students to develop and strengthen their practical computing skills. Equipped with high-performance PCs and essential software tools, the lab supports coursework in programming, software engineering, databases, and web development. It fosters hands-on learning through coding exercises, software simulations, and project development.
Data Communication Lab
The Data Communication Lab offers a hands-on environment for students specializing in computer systems and network engineering. The lab includes routers, switches, and real-world network simulation tools that allow students to design, configure, and troubleshoot wired and wireless networks. It enhances practical understanding of network protocols, data transmission, and network security.
Embedded Lab
The Embedded Systems Lab supports learning in microcontroller-based system development. Students work with hardware platforms such as Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and various sensors to build real-time embedded applications. The lab encourages innovation in robotics, IoT, and hardware programming, bridging the gap between theory and real-world systems.
Cyber Security Lab
The Cyber Security Lab provides a controlled environment to explore network security, ethical hacking, cryptography, and threat analysis. Using advanced software and simulation tools, students gain hands-on experience in penetration testing, securing systems, and understanding cyber threats. The lab nurtures critical thinking and defense strategies to combat modern security challenges.
Robotics Lab
The Robotics Lab empowers students to explore the intersection of computing and mechanical systems. With access to robotic arms, sensors, automation kits, and AI-based components, the lab supports the design, programming, and testing of autonomous systems. It encourages creativity and teamwork in solving real-world challenges through robotics.
Mac Lab
The Mac Lab is tailored for students engaged in creative computing disciplines such as multimedia design, video editing, and mobile app development. Outfitted with high-end Apple devices and industry-standard software, the lab enables seamless development across iOS platforms and supports creativity through visual and UI/UX design projects.
Audio Lab
The Audio Lab serves as a creative space for students working on digital audio production, sound design, and multimedia integration. Equipped with studio-grade equipment and editing software, the lab supports coursework in audio engineering and content creation, helping students build high-quality soundscapes for games, videos, and digital applications.
AR/VR Lab
The Augmented Reality and Virtual Reality Lab introduces students to immersive technologies used in gaming, education, architecture, and healthcare. With VR headsets, motion sensors, and powerful workstations, students develop and test interactive 3D environments, enhancing their skills in Unity, Unreal Engine, and immersive user experiences.
Advanced Computing and Virtualization Lab
The Advanced Computing and Virtualization Lab is designed for exploring cloud technologies, virtualization, and distributed computing. Featuring enterprise-grade servers, virtualization platforms, and cloud simulation tools, the lab enables students to gain practical knowledge in cloud deployment, system scaling, and resource optimization in real-world computing environments.
Sri Lanka Technology Incubator
Conceptnursery, supports start-ups with business mentoring, industry connections, marketing, and financial resources. It provides access to modern labs, advanced networks, and collaboration with SLIIT academics and students on real R&D projects.
Library Learning Commons
Discover a home for curious minds at SLIIT's library network, where flexible spaces, smart tech, and supportive resources come together to fuel your research, writing, and digital growth, enhancing your overall student experience.
