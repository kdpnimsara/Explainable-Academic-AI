WHAT ARE THE PROGRAM OUTCOMES
UQ Bachelor of Commerce Degree offered at SLIIT offers students a world-class business education that combines academic excellence, global exposure, and practical experience. This unique programme allows students to begin their studies at SLIIT in Sri Lanka and complete their degree at the University of Queensland (UQ), one of Australia’s top-ranked universities and a member of the prestigious Group of Eight.
A Commerce degree provides a strong foundation in key business areas such as accounting, finance, economics, and business analytics. Students gain analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making skills that are essential in today’s competitive global economy. The curriculum is designed to be flexible, allowing students to tailor their studies to match their career goals through majors and electives.
At UQ, students benefit from internationally accredited programs (AACSB and EQUIS), access to cutting-edge research, and strong industry connections. The learning experience is enriched through real-world case studies, internships, and opportunities to engage with global business leaders.
This pathway is also cost-effective, offering students the chance to begin their studies locally before transitioning to an internationally recognized qualification. Graduates are highly employable and prepared for careers in banking, consulting, accounting, financial services, and beyond.
Choosing the SLIIT-UQ Commerce pathway means gaining a globally respected degree, developing future-ready business skills, and opening doors to exciting career opportunities around the world.
-
Graduates of the Commerce degree under the SLIIT-UQ pathway are equipped with a strong foundation in business theory, financial literacy, and analytical thinking. The programme develops students’ ability to understand and interpret complex financial data, assess economic trends, and make informed business decisions in a global context.
Students gain in-depth knowledge in core areas such as accounting, finance, economics, and business analytics. They also develop practical skills in data interpretation, financial modeling, and strategic planning. The curriculum emphasizes ethical decision-making, critical thinking, and effective communication—skills essential for success in today’s fast-paced business environment.
Through the University of Queensland’s internationally accredited business school, students benefit from exposure to global business practices, real-world case studies, and industry engagement. They also have opportunities to specialize in areas such as business analytics, international business, or financial planning, tailoring their education to their career goals.
Graduates are well-prepared for careers in banking, accounting, consulting, investment analysis, and corporate finance. The program also provides a strong foundation for professional certifications such as CPA, CFA, or postgraduate studies in business and finance.
Overall, the SLIIT-UQ Commerce programme produces highly employable, globally aware professionals who are ready to contribute meaningfully to organizations across industries and adapt to the evolving demands of the global economy.

FAQs
What is the SLIIT-UQ degree pathway for Bachelor of Commerce?
This pathway allows students to complete the first year of a Commerce degree at SLIIT in Sri Lanka and transfer to the University of Queensland (UQ) in Australia for the final two years, earning a globally recognized UQ degree.
Is it necessary to do an English proficiency test before transferring to Australia?
Yes. Students are required to do an English proficiency test such as IELTS, TOFEL or PTE.
Is there a minimum GPA to be maintained when transferring to the UQ?
Yes, students should maintain 2.7 GPA when transferring to the UQ .
What career opportunities are available after graduation?
Graduates can pursue careers in government, finance, international organizations, consulting, and research. The degree also supports postgraduate study in economics, public policy, or business.
What is the SLIIT-UQ degree pathway?
This pathway allows students to complete the first year of their Economics degree at SLIIT in Sri Lanka and transfer to the University of Queensland (UQ) in Australia for the final two years, earning a globally recognized UQ degree.
