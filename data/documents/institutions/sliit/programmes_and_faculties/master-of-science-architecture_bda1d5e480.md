Master of Science
Architecture
SLIIT's MSc in Architecture programme, in collaboration with Liverpool John Moores University (LJMU) -UK , offers professionals advanced knowledge and practical expertise in architecture, including areas such as architectural design, urban planning, sustainable architecture, and architectural theory. This programme is designed to support career growth while providing a comprehensive understanding of the key elements of modern architectural practice, combining both theoretical foundations and industry-relevant applications to foster architectural excellence.

WHAT ARE THE PROGRAM OUTCOMES
The MSc in Architecture at SLIIT, offered in collaboration with LJMU -UK , is designed to provide an in-depth understanding of the complexities of modern architecture. This programme equips students with advanced skills in areas such as sustainable architecture, design, urban design/ planning, and architectural theory, aligning with the global demands for innovative and environmentally conscious architecture.
By choosing this programme, students benefit from the expertise and academic reputation of LJMU -UK , along with the practical learning environment fostered by SLIIT. Graduates are well-prepared to be merged within the current industry of architecture , and are equipped to address the challenges of the built environment.
Students who wish to pursue higher studies overseas have the potential to continue their education, such as by pursuing a PhD.
The MSc in Architecture programme at SLIIT provides a comprehensive curriculum combining theoretical and practical skills. Core modules include ,
Architectural Design - Focuses on complex architectural design processes, techniques, and strategies , specifically in aspects of conservation & resilient living .
Urban Planning and Design - Emphasizes the integration of architectural design with urban planning concepts & principles .
Sustainable Architecture - Explores eco-friendly and sustainable approaches to modern architectural practices.
Architectural Theory & Research - Provides a historical and contemporary understanding of architectural theory and its application in design practices.
Technology in Architecture: Examines cutting-edge technologies that support modern architectural design and construction.
Upon successful completion of the MSc in Architecture at SLIIT, graduates will have a thorough understanding of advanced architectural principles, theory, and practices. Graduates will be capable of leading architectural projects, designing innovative buildings, and integrating sustainability into urban planning.
The programme fosters critical thinking, problem-solving, and creativity, preparing graduates to address complex architectural challenges effectively. Equipped with these skills, alumni can pursue leadership roles in architecture firms, urban planning, and sustainable design, contributing to the advancement of the architecture and construction industries.

Course Summary
RIBA / SLIA part 1 or equivalent academic qualification in Architecture, with 1 year work experience in an architectural practice or under a chartered architect.
(Applicants are required to submit a digital portfolio or present a portfolio along with practice records at the interview.)
-
The per-semester cost of this course is LKR 500,000. The royalty Fee of £ 1,100 to LJMU at the beginning of Each Year in the form of a bank draft.
An application processing fee of LKR 2,000 will be charged at registration. The course fee is all-inclusive, covering lectures, tutorials, examinations, and access to library and computer laboratory facilities.
Course fees are subject to change.
Fees can be paid to the bank accounts of SLIIT at any branch. For further details on payment options and bank information, please visit:
https://www.sliit.lk/study/fees-and-scholarships/fees-and-costs?#payment-to-sliit
This is a fulltime two-year Master’s degree in Architecture offered by the Liverpool John Moores University in UK at SLIIT School of Architecture in Sri Lanka. The students will directly register with both SLIIT and LJMU for both the years of study since the entire degree programme is offered by LJMU.
Students having a LJMU BSc (Hons) Architecture degree or any other bachelor’s level degree (minimum 3 year) in Architecture are eligible to apply for this MSc Architecture postgraduate degree at SLIIT.
FAQs
What is the duration of the MSc in Architecture program?
The program is structured to be completed over two years, divided into four semesters .
What are the core areas of study in this program?
The curriculum covers the following advanced areas ,
Architectural Design
Urban Design and Planning
Sustainable Architecture
Architectural Theory
Technology in Architecture
Are there flexible study options available for this program?
Yes, s tudents have the opportunity to get the transfer option to LJMU UK to continue their second year , after completing their first year in the SLIIT.
Does this program offer a Graduate Diploma in Architecture?
Yes, students who successfully complete the required modules at the Postgraduate Diploma level can simultaneously achieve the Graduate Diploma in Architecture awarded by SLIIT.
