BSc (Hons)
Architecture
The SLIIT Architecture programme’s first two years match the quality, standards, and outcomes of the Liverpool John Moores University (LJMU) Architecture degree. After successfully completing these two years at SLIIT, students advance to the third year, registering directly with LJMU while continuing their studies at the SLIIT Malabe campus. The third-year curriculum fully aligns with LJMU’s syllabus, ensuring a seamless transition. Upon successful completion, students are awarded the BSc (Hons) Architecture degree directly from LJMU, UK.

Why & What..?
WHY CHOOSE ARCHITECTURE
WHAT YOU WILL STUDY
Choosing the SLIIT Architecture Degree is a smart step for aspiring architects seeking a globally recognized, industry-relevant education. Offered in collaboration with Liverpool John Moores University (LJMU), UK, the programme allows students to complete all three years in Sri Lanka while earning an internationally awarded degree. This not only ensures global exposure but also makes the programme highly cost-effective—approximately 1/ 8th the cost of studying in the UK.
The curriculum is designed to meet the standards of both the Sri Lanka Institute of Architects (SLIA) and the Royal Institute of British Architects (RIBA), ensuring students receive a professional and accredited education. The first two years focus on Sri Lankan architectural context, while the final year introduces global perspectives through LJMU’s curriculum.
Students benefit from one-on-one mentoring by experienced chartered architects and academics, hands-on studio work, and real-world design challenges. The programme also emphasizes critical thinking, sustainability, and digital design tools like AutoCAD and 3D modeling.
With strong academic support, international recognition, and a clear pathway to postgraduate studies or professional practice, SLIIT’s Architecture Degree equips students with the skills, confidence, and credentials to thrive in the global architecture industry.
BSc (Hons) Architecture Degree equips students with a comprehensive foundation in architectural design, theory, and practice, preparing them for both local and international careers. Graduates emerge with a Bachelor of Science (Hons) in Architecture, awarded by Liverpool John Moores University (UK), ensuring global recognition and academic credibility.
The programme’s outcomes align with the standards of the Sri Lanka Institute of Architects (SLIA) and the Royal Institute of British Architects (RIBA), meeting the requirements for a professional Part 1 qualification. Students gain proficiency in architectural design, construction technology, environmental studies, urbanism, and professional practice, along with hands-on skills in AutoCAD, 3D modeling, and freehand drawing.
By the end of the three-year programme, students are capable of conceptualizing and executing innovative architectural solutions that are culturally sensitive and environmentally responsible. They are also prepared for postgraduate studies, including the MSc in Architecture at SLIIT, after completing six months of industry experience under a Chartered Architect.
Graduates are well-positioned to pursue careers in architecture firms, urban planning, interior design, and construction management, or to continue their academic journey toward becoming Chartered Architects. The degree fosters creativity, critical thinking, and global awareness—key traits for success in the architectural profession.

Programme Structure
First Year
Gain fundamental knowledge of architecture, design principles, drawing, and model-making, while understanding cultural, historical, and environmental contexts.
Second Year
Advance your skills through complex design projects, exploring materials, construction techniques, sustainability, and client-oriented solutions.
Third Year
Apply architectural knowledge to real-world challenges with independent projects, research, and professional practice, building a strong portfolio and preparing for the industry.

The first 2 years of this programme is conducted by SLIIT and it is recognized as being equivalent to the first two years of the Liverpool John Moores University architecture degree in terms of quality, standard and the outcome of the programme. Upon the successful completion of the first two years of the SLIIT Architecture bachelor’s programme, students will progress to the third year where they register directly with the Liverpool John Moores University in UK to complete their BSc (Honors) Degree in Architecture while being at the SLIIT School of Architecture in our Malabe campus in Sri Lanka.
Third year syllabus and curriculum conducted at the SLIIT School of Architecture, is completely based on the Liverpool John Moores curriculum and the degree is awarded directly by Liverpool John Moores university, UK at completion of the 3rd year.
FAQs
What degree will I receive upon completing the SLIIT Architecture program?
You will receive a Bachelor of Science (Hons) in Architecture awarded by Liverpool John Moores University (UK). This internationally recognized degree meets the academic requirements for Part 1 of the professional architectural qualification.
Is the SLIIT Architecture degree recognized by professional bodies?
Yes, the program is designed to align with the standards of the Sri Lanka Institute of Architects (SLIA) and the Royal Institute of British Architects (RIBA), ensuring it meets both local and international professional expectations.
What are the career prospects after graduation?
Graduates can pursue careers in architecture firms, urban design, interior architecture, construction management, or continue to postgraduate studies. After six months of industry experience, students can enroll in the MSc in Architecture at SLIIT to work toward becoming Chartered Architects.
What kind of learning experience does the program offer?
The program emphasizes studio-based learning, one-on-one mentoring by Chartered Architects, and hands-on projects. Students gain skills in design thinking, digital modeling, sustainability, and architectural theory, preparing them for real-world challenges.
