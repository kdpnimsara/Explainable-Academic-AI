Step Into the World of Humanities and Sciences
The SLIIT Faculty of Humanities and Sciences at SLIIT is the most diverse faculty on campus, offering programs in Biotechnology, Education, Sciences Law, Mathematics, Nursing, and Psychology. Supported by an expert panel of full-time academics, including PhD holders and professors, the faculty ensures high academic quality and student guidance.
With six state-of-the-art laboratories and a high-tech vertical farming greenhouse, students benefit from hands-on learning and cutting-edge research facilities. Through strong local and international affiliations, students earn globally recognized qualifications. The faculty cultivates well-rounded graduates by encouraging extracurricular involvement and nurturing essential 21st-century skills, ensuring high employability in today’s competitive job market.

Explore Our Facilities
Physics Laboratory
The Physics Laboratory is designed to support Bachelor of Education students specializing in physics teaching. It provides a practical environment to explore core physics concepts such as mechanics, electricity, magnetism, optics, and thermodynamics. Through hands-on experiments and demonstrations, the lab enhances pedagogical skills, scientific reasoning, and instructional confidence, preparing future educators for effective classroom teaching.
Plant Tissue Culture Laboratory
A state-of-the-art laboratory serves as a hub for advanced research in micropropagation, somatic embryogenesis, In vitro Mutagenesis, Somatic Hybridization, doubled haploid plant production, In vitro fertilization, embryo rescue and genetic engineering. Equipped for sterile culture work, it supports undergraduate education, industrial training, and interdisciplinary collaborative research. It plays a pivotal role in nurturing the next generation scientists and entrepreneurs and promoting sustainable agriculture through innovation in plant biotechnology.
Bioscience & Biochemistry Laboratory
A multidisciplinary laboratory fostering the development of future scientists through hands-on exploration in biology, microbiology, biochemistry, and nanotechnology. Equipped with spectrophotometers, centrifuges, incubators, and microscopes, it supports both teaching and research. Emphasizing safety, precision, and scientific inquiry, the laboratory is a cornerstone of the Department of Applied Science’s academic mission.
Advanced Biotechnology Laboratory
An integrated research complex advancing innovation in genetic engineering, molecular diagnostics, and cellular biology. This facility combines molecular biology, cell culture, and instrumentation labs, equipped with PCR systems, electrophoresis units and Gel documentation imaging system, sterile culture environments, and precision analytical tools. It empowers undergraduate and postgraduate training, faculty research, and interdisciplinary collaboration nurturing the next generation of science leaders.
Molecular Biology Laboratory
A specialized facility nurturing future scientists through hands-on training in gene expression, gene editing, nucleic acid extraction, and histological analysis. It includes dedicated labs for molecular biology, staining, and PCR, equipped with advanced tools like thermal cyclers. The complex supports academic instruction and research in molecular genetics, diagnostics, and biotechnology.
Nursing Skills Laboratory
A purpose-built facility designed to empower the healthcare sector by training future nurses through immersive, practice-based learning. Simulating a hospital ward with beds, mannequins, and medical instruments, it enables students to master clinical procedures from basic care to advanced techniques, fostering competence and confidence in real-world healthcare environments.
Physiotherapy Skills Laboratory
A specialized facility designed to build the next generation of physiotherapists through simulation-based education. Equipped with therapeutic tools and rehabilitation equipment, it supports training in physical therapy techniques, musculoskeletal assessments, and patient mobility. The lab fosters confidence, competence, and critical thinking for real-world healthcare practice.
Biomedical Science Laboratory
The Biomedical Science Laboratory is an innovative space in development, purpose-built to inspire and nurture the next generation of biomedical scientists. With a strong emphasis on hands-on, inquiry-based learning, the lab will provide immersive experiences in molecular biology, genetics, biochemistry, immunology etc. - in all of the subjects with a practical component in Biomedical Sciences. Designed to spark curiosity and fuel creativity, this dynamic environment will empower students to explore, experiment, and engage with the scientific process—laying a strong foundation for future discovery and innovation.
The Moot Court
The Moot Court is a specialized facility designed to simulate real-world courtroom experiences for law students. It serves as a practical training ground where aspiring legal professionals can develop advocacy skills, engage in mock trials, and refine their understanding of legal procedures. This space plays a vital role in preparing students for the dynamic world of legal practice.
