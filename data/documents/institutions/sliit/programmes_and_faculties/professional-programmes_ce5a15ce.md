our students
“I’m Methna Kumarage, a proud student of the SLIIT Certificate Program in Practical English. I enrolled in this program shortly after completing my A/L examinations, having come across an advertisement on Facebook that immediately caught my attention. This course has been incredibly beneficial in enhancing all aspects of my English language skills—reading, writing, listening, and especially speaking.
While I’ve always had a solid understanding of grammar and writing, I used to feel nervous when speaking in English.
That’s exactly why I chose this program—to build confidence and fluency in spoken English. I’m truly grateful to our lecturer, for her engaging teaching style and to our course coordinator, for her continuous support throughout the course. I highly recommend this program to anyone who wishes to improve their English skills in a practical, supportive, and enjoyable learning environment”
Ms. Methna Kumarage
