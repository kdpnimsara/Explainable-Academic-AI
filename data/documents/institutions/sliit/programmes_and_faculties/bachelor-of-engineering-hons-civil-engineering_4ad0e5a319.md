Bachelor of Engineering (Hons)
Civil Engineering
With your major in Civil Engineering, you will focus on the areas of infrastructure design, planning, development, construction and maintenance. Students will be able to apply their knowledge through practical work experience and an exciting final year research project that will give them hands-on industry experience. Work as a qualified Professional Civil Engineer in the areas of design, development construction and management in a wide range of fields including structures, transportation, water supply and treatment, and infrastructure.

WHAT ARE THE PROGRAM OUTCOMES
Choosing the UQ Bachelor of Engineering (Hons) in Civil Engineering at SLIIT is a smart investment for students aspiring to become globally recognized civil engineers. This unique pathway allows students to complete the first two years at SLIIT in Sri Lanka and transfer to the University of Queensland (UQ), Australia—a top 50 university globally and a member of Australia’s prestigious Group of Eight.
The programme offers a strong foundation in structural analysis, fluid mechanics, geotechnical engineering, and sustainable infrastructure. Students benefit from hands-on learning, including lab work, surveying camps, and industrial training, ensuring they graduate with both theoretical knowledge and practical experience. The curriculum is designed to meet Engineers Australia accreditation standards, which are internationally recognized under the Washington Accord, enabling graduates to work as professional engineers worldwide.
UQ’s engineering faculty is renowned for cutting-edge research and industry partnerships, giving students access to world-class facilities and global career opportunities. The program also emphasizes sustainability, innovation, and ethical engineering practices—skills that are increasingly vital in today’s infrastructure and environmental challenges.
With a blend of local affordability and international prestige, this degree equips students with the skills, credentials, and confidence to lead in civil engineering fields such as structural design, water resources, transportation, and environmental engineering. Whether aiming to work in Sri Lanka or abroad, the SLIIT–UQ Civil Engineering degree is a launchpad for a successful, impactful career.
Graduates of the University of Queensland Bachelor of Engineering (Hons) Civil degree are equipped with a comprehensive set of technical, analytical, and professional skills that prepare them for success in the global engineering industry. The programme is designed to produce engineers who are not only proficient in core civil engineering disciplines but also capable of addressing complex infrastructure challenges with innovative and sustainable solutions.
Upon completion, students will demonstrate strong competencies in structural engineering, geotechnical analysis, fluid mechanics, transportation systems, and environmental engineering. They will be able to design, analyze, and manage civil engineering projects using modern tools and technologies, including CAD software, simulation platforms, and data analysis tools.
The programme emphasizes problem-solving, critical thinking, and project management, enabling graduates to lead multidisciplinary teams and deliver engineering solutions that are safe, efficient, and environmentally responsible. Students also develop a deep understanding of engineering ethics, sustainability, and the societal impact of infrastructure development, preparing them to make informed and responsible decisions in their careers.
Graduates are trained to communicate effectively with diverse stakeholders, work collaboratively in team environments, and adapt to evolving industry standards and technologies. With the program’s international accreditation through Engineers Australia and recognition under the Washington Accord, students are qualified to pursue professional engineering careers not only in Sri Lanka and Australia but also across the globe.
In essence, the UQ Civil Engineering degree at SLIIT produces future-ready engineers who are technically skilled, globally competitive, and committed to building a better world.

FAQs
What is the structure of the SLIIT–UQ Civil Engineering degree?
The program is structured as a 2+2 pathway, where students complete the first two years at SLIIT and transfer to the University of Queensland (UQ) in Australia for the final two years. Upon completion, students receive a Bachelor of Engineering ( Honours ) in Civil Engineering from UQ.
Is the degree internationally recognized?
Yes. The degree is accredited by Engineers Australia and recognized under the Washington Accord, allowing graduates to practice as professional engineers in many countries worldwide.
Can this degree be completed in Sri Lanka?
No. Students should transfer to Australia for the final 2 years of the studies.
What career opportunities are available after graduation?
Graduates can pursue careers in structural engineering, transportation, water resources, geotechnical engineering, and construction management. They are qualified to work in both the public and private sectors, in Sri Lanka, Australia, and globally.
