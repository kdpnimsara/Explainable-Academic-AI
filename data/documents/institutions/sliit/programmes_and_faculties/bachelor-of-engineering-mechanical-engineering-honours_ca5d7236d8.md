Bachelor of Engineering (Hons)
Mechanical Engineering
Mechanical engineering is a discipline that is recognized worldwide. As one of the broadest engineering disciplines, it will provide you with versatile skills for numerous career options. System conception, design, manufacturing, maintenance, and management are all within the scope of mechanical engineering. These systems include micromechanical devices, power-generating turbines, thermal power generation, and air and transport systems.
This course is oriented to provide skills development opportunities with hands-on experience. You will learn how to apply your knowledge and skills to devise and/or develop solutions for a range of engaging and challenging industry problems.
In particular, you will appreciate applying your multidisciplinary problem-solving skills across a spectrum of science and engineering endeavours that extend through to biomedical engineering. In your final year, you will undertake an individual design or research project.

WHAT ARE THE PROGRAM OUTCOMES
Choosing Mechanical Engineering through the SLIIT-UQ pathway offers a unique blend of academic excellence, global exposure, and practical experience. This dual-institution programme allows students to begin their studies at SLIIT in Sri Lanka and complete their degree at the prestigious University of Queensland (UQ), ranked among the world’s top universities for engineering 1.
Mechanical Engineering is one of the most versatile and in-demand disciplines, covering everything from robotics and aerospace to energy systems and biomedical devices. At UQ, students gain access to cutting-edge facilities, including advanced laboratories and innovation hubs, where they can apply theoretical knowledge to real-world challenges. The curriculum emphasizes design, manufacturing, thermodynamics, and automation, preparing graduates for diverse careers in industries such as automotive, aerospace, energy, and healthcare.
The SLIIT-UQ pathway also fosters global competencies. Students benefit from international teaching standards, multicultural collaboration, and industry-linked projects that enhance employability. UQ’s strong ties with industry leaders and its vibrant student community provide networking opportunities and career support that extend well beyond graduation.
Moreover, this pathway is cost-effective, allowing students to complete part of their degree locally before transitioning to an internationally recognized qualification. It’s an ideal choice for aspiring engineers who seek a world-class education, hands-on experience, and a competitive edge in the global job market.
Graduates of the Mechanical Engineering programme under the SLIIT-UQ pathway emerge with a globally recognized qualification and a robust foundation in engineering principles, design, and innovation. This programme equips students with the ability to apply advanced knowledge in mechanics, thermodynamics, materials science, and control systems to solve real-world engineering problems.
Students develop strong analytical and problem-solving skills through hands-on projects, simulations, and industry-linked experiences. They gain proficiency in using modern engineering tools and software, enabling them to design, analyze, and optimize mechanical systems ranging from engines and robotics to HVAC and manufacturing systems.

FAQs
What is the SLIIT-UQ pathway for Mechanical Engineering?
The SLIIT-UQ pathway allows students to complete the first two years of their Mechanical Engineering degree at SLIIT in Sri Lanka and transfer to the University of Queensland (UQ) in Australia to complete the final two years, earning a globally recognized UQ degree.
Is there a minimum GPA to be maintained when transferring to the UQ?
Yes, students should maintain 2.7GPA when transferring to the UQ.
What are the benefits of choosing this pathway?
Students benefit from a cost-effective start at SLIIT, followed by access to UQ’s world-class facilities, international faculty, and industry connections. This pathway enhances global exposure, practical skills, and employability in diverse engineering sectors.
What career opportunities are available after graduation?
Graduates can pursue careers in automotive, aerospace, energy, manufacturing, robotics, and biomedical industries. The UQ degree is internationally accredited, opening doors to global job markets and postgraduate study options.
