Faculty Of Engineering
Facilities
Innovative Engineering Facilities
The Faculty of Engineering features cutting-edge laboratories, fully equipped workshops, and simulation centers across disciplines like civil, mechanical, electrical, and mechatronics engineering. These facilities provide students with practical exposure to real-world tools and technologies.
Learning environments are designed to support project work, experimentation, and collaborative problem-solving, enabling students to innovate and apply engineering principles effectively.
Advanced Engineering Labs
Outfitted with industry-grade tools and modern equipment.
Specialized Research Facilities
Dedicated spaces for robotics, electronics, and automation.
Smart Classrooms
Technology-enabled learning environments with interactive systems.
Project & Innovation Zones
Collaborative areas to design, prototype, and test engineering ideas.
Student Accessibility
Extended access hours to support projects and practical work.
