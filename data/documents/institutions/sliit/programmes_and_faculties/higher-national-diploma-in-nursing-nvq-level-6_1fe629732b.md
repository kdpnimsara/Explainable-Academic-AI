WHAT ARE THE PROGRAM OUTCOMES
The Higher National Diploma in Nursing at SLIIT offers a well-rounded, three-year programme designed to develop the knowledge and skills needed to excel in modern healthcare. Delivered by a team of experienced nursing professionals, the programme combines theoretical learning with hands-on practice in our advanced nursing skills lab.
Students gain real-world experience through clinical training at leading hospitals in Sri Lanka, ensuring they build strong clinical competencies. Supported by SLIIT’s interactive Learning Management System, this programme prepares you for a rewarding career in nursing, locally and globally.
The programme covers a balanced mix of theoretical knowledge, in-house practice, and clinical experience. Students learn foundational sciences, professional nursing concepts, and clinical modules through classroom and lab sessions. Practical training includes demonstrations of key nursing procedures, guided self-practice, and return demonstrations to lecturers for feedback.
Once proficient, students undertake clinical placements at hospitals to apply their skills in real-life scenarios. This structured approach ensures you acquire a deep understanding of nursing theory, practical techniques, and professional responsibilities needed for success in diverse healthcare settings.
Graduates of this programme develop the ability to deliver safe, evidence-based, and compassionate care across varied healthcare environments. You will learn to integrate theory with practice, demonstrate effective communication and leadership, and work collaboratively within multidisciplinary teams.
The programme also enhances critical thinking, problem-solving, and decision-making abilities while fostering resilience, adaptability, and ethical sensitivity. With strong managerial and technological skills, graduates are well-prepared to contribute meaningfully to the healthcare sector, maintain professional networks, and pursue lifelong learning, ensuring continued growth in their nursing career.

FAQs
How long is the Higher National Diploma in Nursing program at SLIIT?
The program spans four years, covering NVQ Levels 4, 5, and 6, with comprehensive theoretical, practical, and clinical training.
Where do students gain their clinical experience during the program?
Students complete over 2,600 hours of clinical placements in leading hospitals across Sri Lanka.
What qualifications will I receive after completing this program?
Upon successful completion, students are awarded the Higher National Diploma in Nursing (NVQ Level 6), recognized locally and internationally.
What career opportunities are available after completing this program?
Graduates can work as professional nurses in hospitals, clinics, community health centers, and pursue further studies or overseas opportunities.
