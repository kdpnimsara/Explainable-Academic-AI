Step Into the Future of Architecture
Welcome to the SLIIT School of Architecture, where creativity and functionality meet to shape the future of the built environment. Our program is tailored to equip students with a deep understanding of architectural theory, design, sustainability, and urban planning. Through hands-on studio work, model-making, and digital tools, students bring ideas to life while gaining exposure to real-world projects and leading professionals in the field.
The curriculum is designed to balance artistic innovation with technical excellence, encouraging critical thinking and cultural sensitivity. Our graduates are prepared to lead in architectural design, urban development, and construction consultancy, both locally and internationally.

Explore Our Facilities
Design Studios
Spacious studios that foster collaborative and individual design work.
These 24 hours accessible studios are the heart of architectural learning at SLIIT, equipped for Architectural Drafting/Drawing/ Sketching, conceptualization, and model-based design. Students engage in hands-on projects, critiques, and collaborative learning in this dynamic space that mirrors a professional studio environment.
Key Features:
1. Individual and group workspaces
2. Aided facility for Tutorials/Lectures/Workshops
3. Whiteboards/ Pin-up walls for reviews
4. Projection facilities
5. Direct access to critique rooms
CAD Lab
A Computer lab supporting CAD, 3D modelling, and rendering applications.
This facility supports advanced digital design drafting and visualization. Equipped with the latest software and updated machines, the CAD Lab empowers students to develop skills in drafting, simulation, parametric modelling, and digital fabrication tools essential for contemporary architecture.
Key Features:
1. Licensed AutoCAD, Revit
2. High-speed internet
3. Plotters and 3D printers
4. Technical support staff
5. Software tutorials
Crit Room / Smart Lecture Room
A multifunctional space designed for presentations, reviews, lectures and forums etc.
This room serves as the formal setting for student design reviews and critiques. It is also used for lectures, guest sessions, and exhibitions. The flexible arrangement enables diverse pedagogical approaches in design learning.
Key Features:
1. Projector and screen
2. Moveable display panels
3. Whiteboards and pin-up space
4. Sound system
5. Guest seating
6. Smart Recording capability
Common Study Areas
Relaxed, informal zones that support tutorials, individual and collaborative learning.
These shared spaces are ideal for majorly tutoring, quiet study, design discussions, and design experimentation. Strategically located, they serve as breathing spaces for idea exchange and design project development.
Key Features:
1. Lounge seating
2. Aided facility for Tutorials Workshops
3. Wi-Fi access
4. Student display areas
5. Accessibility features
Library Learning Commons

Research Facilities
Experience groundbreaking research with modern specialized equipment, strong IT infrastructure, and dedicated centres promoting interdisciplinary collaboration. Enjoy core facilities while prioritizing ethics, safety, and innovation.
Student Transport
SLIIT's dedicated shuttle services run across multiple routes, linking the campus with residential areas to ensure easy access. Designed with safety and convenience in mind, they enhance student mobility and make daily travel hassle-free.
Student Services
SLIIT student services provide comprehensive support through academic advising, learning resources, and career guidance while empowering students to thrive both academically and professionally.
Gymnasium
SLIIT's gymnasium acts as a vital hub for student well-being and community activities, supporting physical health, mental clarity, and social connection, enriching the student experience by encouraging a balanced, active lifestyle.
SLIIT Sports Facilities
With over 22 sports to choose from, SLIIT promotes active lifestyles , social interaction and stress management. These opportunities nurture personal growth, skill development, and a well-rounded university experience.
Laboratory Facilities
SLIIT's cutting-edge laboratories transform theory into practice, offering hands-on learning, skill development, and innovation. These advanced facilities drive research, enhance engagement, and equip students for future careers.
Drawing Studio
Step into SLIIT's modern drawing studio to sharpen your technical skills, explore creativity, and develop critical thinking. It is a space designed for personal expression, stress relief, and enhancing your overall well-being.
Classrooms
Moving away from the traditional lecture-hall format, SLIIT provides dynamic, interactive, and technology-driven classrooms, ensuring enhanced engagement and improved learning outcomes to prepare students for the digital workplace.
Auditorium
SLIIT's high-tech auditori um host s a range of events, from large lectures to cultural performances, strengthening its reputation and promoting interdisciplinary learning while expanding its global reach.
Recreation Centre
SLIIT Recreation Centre creates a supportive environment where students can enhance their physical, mental, and social well-being, contributing to a fulfilling university experience and academic success
Clubs & Societies
Explore SLIIT's dynamic clubs and societies that spark leadership, personal growth, and valuable career networks. Join, build soft skills, and elevate your university journey through meaningful connections and experiences.
SLIIT Open Air Theatre
A centerpiece of campus life, the SLIIT Open Air Theatre hosts student performances, educational programs, and special events. Its open and welcoming environment encourages creativity, talent, and meaningful connections while enriching the overall student experience.
