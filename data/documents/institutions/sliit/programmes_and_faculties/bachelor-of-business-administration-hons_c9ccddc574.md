The BBA programme at SLIIT, awarded by Liverpool John Moores University (UK), equips students with a strong foundation in business principles. The curriculum covers key areas like management, marketing, finance, and organizational behavior . Students will gain practical skills through case studies, group projects, and internships, ensuring they are prepared for real-world business challenges. The programme also offers opportunities for global exposure and leadership development, preparing graduates for successful careers in various industries.

WHAT ARE THE PROGRAM OUTCOMES
Studying the BBA programme of Liverpool John Moores University (LJMU) offers a unique opportunity to develop a diverse skill set suitable for multiple business roles. The programme integrates advanced business simulation tools and live consultancy projects, enabling students to apply theoretical knowledge to real-world business challenges. This hands-on approach ensures that students are well-equipped with practical business skills, preparing them for a successful career in various industries.
Graduates of this programme will earn a globally recognized Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) award, a testament to the technical expertise they acquire throughout their studies. Furthermore, LJMU's commitment to academic excellence is highlighted by its shortlisting for the prestigious Times Higher Education Awards 2024 Business School of the Year, further reinforcing the quality and reputation of the institution. This combination of practical experience, academic rigor, and industry recognition makes this course a strong foundation for future business leaders.
The Business Management programme offers a comprehensive curriculum that combines modern business theory with the practical application of essential business skills. As a student in this programme, you will gain proficiency in a range of critical areas, including digital analytical expertise , which equips you to interpret data and leverage technology for informed decision-making. You will also develop the ability to manage finances effectively using the appropriate tools and techniques and gain a solid understanding of financial markets and their impact on business operations.
Additionally, the programme emphasizes key skills in people management, enabling you to lead and motivate teams, as well as the development and marketing of products and services to meet consumer demands.
Students will also be trained in planning and project management, ensuring successful delivery of business initiatives. Upon graduation, you will be well-prepared to tackle the evolving challenges of global business, adapting to diverse markets and dynamic environments.
Upon successful completion of the Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) programme, graduates will:
• Apply key business concepts, including management theories, strategic planning, marketing, finance, and entrepreneurship, to real-world business scenarios.
• Analyze contemporary business challenges and develop appropriate solutions using critical thinking and problem-solving skills.
• Demonstrate effective communication and leadership skills, collaborating in teams and making informed decisions.
• Foster innovation and entrepreneurial thinking to identify opportunities and develop sustainable business models.
• Utilize digital tools and business analytics to manage complex data and make data-driven business decisions.
• Apply research methodologies to enhance decision-making and business strategies.
• Understand and implement ethical considerations and corporate social responsibility in business practices.
• Adapt to dynamic market conditions and continuously engage in learning to remain relevant in a changing business environment.
• Prepare for diverse career opportunities in management, marketing, finance, and entrepreneurship, with the ability to pursue further academic advancements in the field of business.
This comprehensive skill set ensures that graduates are well-prepared to thrive in the modern business world.

Programme Structure
Year 1
Focus on core business principles like management, accounting, marketing, and economics, with an emphasis on communication, teamwork, and critical thinking through case studies and practical learning.
Year 2
Build on foundational knowledge with advanced topics in financial management , HR, strategic management, and entrepreneurship, while developing leadership, analytical, and research skills for managerial roles.
Year 3
Specialize in strategic decision-making, sustainable practices, and cross-cultural management, preparing students for leadership roles with a focus on global business and project management skills.

The programme will be available upon meeting the minimum student enrolment.
LJMU Entry Requirements:
- Completion of the SLIIT Higher Diploma, including the Bridging Module in Research Methodology
- A minimum of a "C" pass in GCE O/L English (or equivalent)
Applicants who have successfully completed the SLIIT Foundation Programme are eligible for enrolment into the programme. Applicants who have completed foundation programmes from other institutions may be considered subject to approval by the Institute Senate.

The fee per semester for this 3-year degree is LKR. 340,000. Each year consists of 2 semesters. Additionally, £ 1,100 will be charged in the final year. Fees for each semester must be paid before commencement of the semester.
The course fee is all-inclusive, covering lectures, tutorials, examinations, and access to library and computer laboratory facilities.
Course fees are subject to change.
Fees can be paid to the bank accounts of SLIIT at any branch. For further details on payment options and bank information, please visit:

At SLIIT, we employ an active blended learning approach, providing a combination of face - to -face and online learning throughout your academic journey. This approach ensures a dynamic and varied learning experience, enabling you to engage fully with your studies. By blending traditional classroom interactions with digital learning, we create a flexible and accessible environment that supports your individual learning needs. Our method allows
students to easily connect with module leaders for guidance, whether through in-person meetings on campus or virtual consultations via video call.
The programme's structure includes a balanced mix of formal lectures, informal workshops, online activities, and seminars. This diverse delivery method encourages students to actively engage in discussions, refine their understanding of key theories, and apply these concepts in practical settings. Additionally, we invite professional speakers from industry to share their experiences and perspectives with students. These guest speakers offer valuable insights into the real-world application of business principles and provide a practical understanding of the workplace, helping students bridge the gap between theory and practice. Through this comprehensive approach, students are well-prepared to tackle the challenges of their future careers while gaining relevant and up-to-date knowledge.
FAQs
What career opportunities are available after completing this degree?
Graduates of this program will be well-equipped to pursue diverse career paths in the business sector. Potential roles include, but are not limited to:
Business Analyst
Marketing Executive
Human Resource Manager
Financial Analyst
Entrepreneur
Sales Manager
Operations Manager
Digital Marketing Specialist
Management Consultant
Project Manager
Can I pursue the BBA (Hons) degree offered by SLIIT while studying for the LJMU BBA degree?
Yes, it is possible to pursue the BBA (Hons) degree offered by SLIIT while also completing the LJMU BBA degree. As an LJMU-registered student, you have access to the double degree pathway. Many students have successfully completed both degrees concurrently.
