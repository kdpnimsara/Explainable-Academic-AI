Facilities at Faculty of Humanities & Sciences
State-of-the-art labs and workshops for engineering excellence
Faculty Of Humanities Sciences
Facilities
State-of-the-Art Learning Spaces
The Faculty of Humanities and Sciences at SLIIT offers a wide range of advanced facilities that foster experiential learning, interdisciplinary research, and industry-relevant skill development. From well-equipped Physics and Bioscience laboratories to specialized facilities in Molecular Biology, Biotechnology, Nursing, and Physiotherapy, our students gain practical exposure in real-world environments.
Facilities such as the Plant Tissue Culture Lab and the Moot Court further enhance academic and professional readiness, supporting both scientific discovery and legal education. These spaces are designed to inspire innovation and prepare students for impactful careers.
Science Laboratories
Well-equipped labs for hands-on learning in Biology, Chemistry, and Physics.

Specialized facilities supporting skill development and research.
