WHAT ARE THE PROGRAM OUTCOMES
Choosing the University of Queensland (UQ) Electrical Engineering degree at SLIIT offers students a unique opportunity to gain a world-class engineering education while beginning their studies in Sri Lanka. This 2+2 pathway programme allows students to complete the first two years at SLIIT and transfer to UQ—ranked among the top 50 universities globally and a member of Australia’s prestigious Group of Eight.
The programme provides a strong foundation in electrical circuits, electronics, signal processing, control systems, and power engineering, with a focus on real-world applications and innovation. Students benefit from hands-on labs, project-based learning, and exposure to cutting-edge technologies that prepare them for the demands of modern electrical engineering careers.
UQ’s Bachelor of Engineering ( Honours ) is accredited by Engineers Australia and recognized under the Washington Accord, enabling graduates to work as professional engineers in over 20 countries. This global recognition opens doors to careers in renewable energy, telecommunications, robotics, embedded systems, and smart infrastructure.
The programme also emphasizes sustainability, ethical engineering, and leadership, ensuring graduates are not only technically skilled but also socially responsible. With access to UQ’s world-class research facilities and industry partnerships, students gain valuable experience and networking opportunities that enhance their employability.
In short, the SLIIT–UQ Electrical Engineering degree combines academic excellence, international recognition, and practical experience, making it an ideal choice for students aiming to power the future of technology.
Graduates of the University of Queensland (UQ) Electrical Engineering degree are equipped with the knowledge, skills, and global recognition needed to thrive in a rapidly evolving technological world. The programme is designed to produce engineers who are not only technically proficient but also innovative, ethical, and industry-ready .
Students develop a deep understanding of electrical systems, electronics, signal processing, control systems, embedded systems, and power engineering. They gain hands-on experience through labs, simulations, and real-world projects, enabling them to design, analyze, and implement complex electrical and electronic systems.
The programme emphasizes problem-solving, critical thinking, and systems-level design, preparing graduates to tackle challenges in areas such as renewable energy, smart grids, robotics, telecommunications, and automation. Students also learn to use industry-standard tools and software, enhancing their technical fluency and adaptability.
Graduates are trained to work effectively in multidisciplinary teams, communicate technical ideas clearly, and manage engineering projects with a focus on sustainability and societal impact. The programme’s accreditation by Engineers Australia and recognition under the Washington Accord ensures that graduates are eligible for professional engineering roles in over 20 countries.
By the end of the programme, students are not only ready for employment but also well-prepared for further study, research, or entrepreneurship in the field of electrical engineering. The SLIIT–UQ pathway offers a powerful combination of local accessibility and international excellence, making it a launchpad for a successful global engineering career.

FAQs
What is the structure of the UQ Electrical Engineering degree at SLIIT?
The program follows a 2+2 model, where students complete the first two years at SLIIT and transfer to the University of Queensland (UQ) in Australia for the final two years. Graduates receive a Bachelor of Engineering ( Honours ) in Electrical Engineering from UQ.
Is the degree internationally recognized?
Yes. The degree is accredited by Engineers Australia and recognized under the Washington Accord, allowing graduates to work as professional engineers in over 20 countries, including the UK, USA, Canada, and Japan.
Is it necessary to do an English proficiency test before transferring to Australia?
Yes. Students are required to do an English proficiency test such as IELTS, TOFEL or PTE.
What career opportunities are available after graduation?
Graduates can pursue careers in power systems, telecommunications, robotics, embedded systems, automation, and renewable energy. They are well-prepared for roles in both the public and private sectors, locally and internationally.
