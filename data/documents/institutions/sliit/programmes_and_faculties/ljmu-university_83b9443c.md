Liverpool John Moores University(LJMU) is a modern UK university known for innovation and industry-linked education. Through SLIIT, students can pursue degrees in areas like Business, Law, Quantity Surveying, Architecture, and Interior Design. Delivered locally, these programmes combine practical skills with academic excellence. Students benefit from affordable tuition, supportive guidance, and qualifications that open doors globally.

FAQs
Can I complete the LJMU degrees at SLIIT?
Yes. Students can complete all three years of their LJMU degree at SLIIT
Can I transfer to the UK during my studies?
Yes. Students can transfer to the UK campus after completing either the first or second year of their LJMU program at SLIIT.
Are scholarships available for LJMU students?
Yes. Merit-based and sports scholarships are available at enrollment. Additional scholarships are also offered when transferring to the UK.
What support is provided for students?
SLIIT provides academic tutoring, admin assistance, mental health counselling, career guidance, and support for students with disabilities.
