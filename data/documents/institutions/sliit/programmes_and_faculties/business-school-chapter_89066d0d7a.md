Welcome to the SLIIT Business School Alumni Association
The SLIIT Business School Alumni Association unites graduates from all SLIIT campuses and its partner universities. With over 10,000 alumni across the globe, the association stands as a proud pillar of professional excellence and community support.
Its mission is to become the strongest, most widespread alumni network dedicated to uplifting both its members and SLIIT. Through mentoring, events, and collaborative initiatives, the association fosters lifelong relationships and creates opportunities for personal and professional advancement. As ambassadors of SLIIT, alumni contribute meaningfully to society while inspiring future generations of students to achieve success.
