Fashion Business & Management
The Fashion Business & Management programme at SLIIT, awarded by Manchester Metropolitan University, UK, prepares students for careers in the dynamic fashion industry. The curriculum covers key areas such as fashion marketing, retail management, fashion merchandising, and brand strategy. Students gain practical insights through industry collaborations, projects, and internships, ensuring they are well-equipped for managerial roles in fashion businesses, retail companies, and global fashion markets.

WHAT ARE THE PROGRAM OUTCOMES
The BSc (Hons) Fashion Business and Management programme is designed to meet the demands of the rapidly evolving global fashion industry, where customer preferences drive businesses to think and act more strategically.
By joining this programme, you will embark on a transformative journey that places you at the forefront of contemporary industry thinking, equipping you with the skills to excel as a graduate and future leader. Today’s successful fashion companies prioritize customer experience, integrating marketing, buying, merchandising, data analytics, digital channels, and in-store operations to create seamless, customer-centric services. This dynamic environment requires graduates with strong entrepreneurial vision, business acumen, and marketing expertise to thrive in the industry.
Our curriculum approaches the fashion industry from a business management perspective, ensuring that students develop a deep understanding of strategic decision-making and innovative problem-solving.
You will learn from a dedicated team of experienced educators and researchers, each an expert in their field. Additionally, we collaborate with industry professionals, providing valuable insights and networking opportunities to bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world application . This program is designed to produce forward-thinking graduates who can adapt to and drive change in the fast-paced fashion business landscape.
This programme offers a comprehensive yet specialized approach to understanding the future of the fashion industry. It is designed to develop students' creative thinking, strategic insight, and commercial acumen, ensuring they are well-equipped to navigate the evolving global fashion landscape.
Students will gain invaluable knowledge across key areas essential for success in the industry. This includes global business strategy, enabling them to understand market dynamics and competitive positioning. They will explore digital-driven marketing, learning how to leverage social media, e-commerce, and emerging technologies to engage customers effectively .
Additionally, the course covers finance and sustainability, providing insights into responsible business practices and ethical decision-making. Students will also study supply chain management, learning how to optimize production and distribution processes. Through data analytics and innovative entrepreneurship, they will develop the ability to interpret market trends, make informed business decisions, and drive innovation, preparing them for leadership roles in the fashion industry.
Upon successful completion of the programme, graduates will,
Apply knowledge of fashion business principles, marketing, and supply chain management to drive strategic decisions in the fashion industry.
Analyze consumer behavior , market trends, and sales data to develop data-driven business strategies.
Implement innovative sourcing and production strategies while ensuring ethical and sustainable business practices.
Develop comprehensive marketing strategies to enhance brand positioning and customer engagement.
Utilize digital tools and analytics to optimize fashion business operations and decision-making processes.
Assess the economic, cultural, and societal impacts of fashion business decisions in a global context.
Demonstrate leadership, teamwork, and communication skills in multidisciplinary and multicultural business environments.
Uphold professional and ethical standards in the fashion and apparel industry while adapting to evolving industry trends.
Engage in lifelong learning and continuous professional development to stay relevant in the ever-changing fashion business landscape.

This dynamic and industry-focused programme offers a comprehensive and immersive learning experience designed to equip students with cutting-edge skills essential for the ever- evolving fashion business landscape. Through project-based assignments, students will engage in real-world challenges, developing both individual and group projects that encourage collaboration, innovation, and strategic thinking. The curriculum places a strong emphasis on creative and analytical communication, incorporating infographics, data visualization, digital content creation, and video production to enhance storytelling and business presentations.
Students will gain hands-on experience in developing business and marketing plans, refining their entrepreneurial mindset, and mastering the art of business pitching to industry professionals. The program is enriched with exclusive industry connections, offering live briefs, competitions, and masterclasses led by renowned experts. Additionally, students will benefit from guest lectures by industry leaders, providing invaluable insights into global market trends and business strategies. The project-based live briefs will challenge students to apply their learning in real-time scenarios, preparing them for high-impact careers.
With a strong focus on experiential learning and industry collaboration, this program ensures that graduates are well-prepared to thrive in the competitive world of fashion business and management, equipped with the skills to drive innovation and success.

What are the options available for students in the top-up year?
Students have two pathways to complete their final year of study.
Option 1: Students can complete the first two years (Level 4 C 5) at SLIIT in Sri Lanka and transfer to Manchester Metropolitan University (MMU) in the UK to complete their final year at the Manchester campus.
Option 2: Students can complete the full three-year program at SLIIT in Sri Lanka.
Both options lead to the BSc (Hons) Fashion Business & Management degree, awarded by Manchester Metropolitan University.
Can I directly enter the top-up year if I already have a diploma qualification?
Yes, a lateral entry option is available. If you hold a National Diploma from a recognized university or institute, and your qualification is equivalent to a UK DipHE or HND (Level 5), you may be considered for direct entry into the top-up year. Eligibility will be assessed on a case-by-case basis in accordance with Manchester Metropolitan University’s Recruitment and Admissions Policy.
What are the progression pathway with MMU
Students will follow the SLIIT curriculum during the first and second years of study, leading to the award of a Higher Diploma qualification comprising 60 SLIIT credits. Upon successful completion of the Higher Diploma, students will be eligible to register with MMU for the Top-Up (final) year of study, leading to the award of the BSc (Hons) Fashion Business & Management.
