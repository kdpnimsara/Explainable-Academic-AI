WHAT ARE THE PROGRAM OUTCOMES
The Bachelor of Artificial Intelligence offered by Deakin University and delivered at SLIIT is Sri Lanka’s first Australian AI degree programme, providing students with a globally recognized qualification while studying locally. This innovative three-year, full-time programme equips students with the skills to design, develop, and deploy intelligent systems using the latest advancements in artificial intelligence.
Students gain hands-on experience in areas such as machine learning, data science, computer vision, and natural language processing. The curriculum mirrors that of Deakin University in Australia, ensuring the same academic quality and industry relevance. Deakin academics visit SLIIT each semester to deliver in-person teaching, enhancing the international learning experience.
The programme also emphasizes employability, offering professional work placements and access to cutting-edge labs and software. Students can earn additional certifications, such as Microsoft Azure AI credentials, boosting their career prospects.
Graduates are well-prepared for roles like AI engineer, data scientist, AI ethicist, and AI architect across various industries including healthcare, finance, and technology. With a strong foundation in both theory and practical application, students are empowered to solve real-world problems using AI.
Choosing the SLIIT–Deakin Bachelor of Artificial Intelligence degree is a strategic decision for students aiming to thrive in the rapidly evolving tech landscape. This programme offers the unique advantage of earning a prestigious Australian degree from Deakin University while studying in Sri Lanka, significantly reducing the cost of international education without compromising on quality.
The curriculum is designed to meet global industry standards, covering cutting-edge topics such as machine learning, robotics, computer vision, and natural language processing. Students benefit from hands-on learning, real-world projects, and access to state-of-the-art AI labs. Deakin’s academic staff collaborate with SLIIT to deliver modules, ensuring a consistent and high-quality learning experience.
What sets this program apart is its strong industry focus. Students are encouraged to pursue internships and certifications, including Microsoft Azure AI credentials, enhancing their employability. The program also emphasizes ethical AI development, preparing graduates to tackle real-world challenges responsibly.
SLIIT’s vibrant campus life, expert faculty, and supportive learning environment further enrich the student experience. With growing global demand for AI professionals, graduates are well-positioned for careers as AI engineers, data scientists, and AI consultants in sectors like healthcare, finance, and technology.
In essence, the SLIIT–Deakin AI degree combines international recognition, practical skills, and affordability, making it an ideal choice for ambitious students ready to shape the future with artificial intelligence.
Graduates of the SLIIT–Deakin Bachelor of Artificial Intelligence degree emerge with a robust set of skills and competencies that align with global industry demands. The programme is designed to produce AI professionals who are not only technically proficient but also ethically grounded and innovation-driven .
Upon completion, students will have a deep understanding of core AI principles, including machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, and natural language processing. They will be able to design, develop, and deploy intelligent systems that solve complex real- world problems across various domains such as healthcare, finance, robotics, and smart cities. A key outcome of the programme is the ability to analyze and interpret large datasets, enabling data-driven decision-making. Students also gain proficiency in programming languages and tools commonly used in AI development, such as Python, TensorFlow, and cloud-based platforms like Microsoft Azure.
The curriculum emphasizes critical thinking, problem-solving, and research skills, preparing students to contribute to cutting-edge AI innovations. Graduates are also trained in the ethical and societal implications of AI, ensuring they can develop responsible and inclusive technologies.
Additionally, the programme fosters strong communication and teamwork abilities, essential for collaborative environments in tech-driven industries. With exposure to real-world projects and industry placements, students graduate with practical experience and a competitive edge in the job market.
In essence, the programme equips students to become future-ready AI professionals, capable of leading and shaping the next wave of technological transformation.

FAQs
What is the duration and structure of the SLIIT–Deakin AI degree?
The program is a three-year full-time degree offered in collaboration with Deakin University, Australia. Students have the flexibility to either complete all three years of the degree at SLIIT in their home country or transfer to Deakin University in Australia and complete , with Deakin academics delivering key modules to ensure international standards and consistency with the Australian curriculum.
Can a student transfer to Australia while studying?
Students can transfer to Australia any given point of 3 years they are studying the Deakin University Bachelor of AI degree.
What kind of career opportunities does this degree offer?
Graduates are prepared for roles such as:
AI Engineer
Data Scientist
Machine Learning Specialist
AI Consultant
They can work in industries like healthcare, finance, robotics, and smart technology, both locally and internationally.
Is the degree internationally recognized?
Yes. The degree is awarded by Deakin University, a top-ranked Australian institution. It is recognized globally and provides students with the same academic credentials as those studying in Australia.
