Make a Difference Through Your Support
Your support in many meaningful ways can truly make a difference in someone’s life and future. Every contribution — whether big or small — helps strengthen academic excellence, foster student development, and drive research and innovation at SLIIT.
Together, we can create greater opportunities, empower the next generation of leaders, and make a lasting impact on higher education and society. By joining hands, we continue to build a brighter, more inclusive, and inspiring future for all.

Support to SLIIT
Help Shape the Future
Our support in many ways can make a real difference in someone’s life. Whether it’s time, knowledge, opportunities, or resources, every act of support contributes to the growth of SLIIT and its students. Together, we can build a stronger future through education, innovation, and shared values.
Being part of the SLIIT community means you can inspire the next generation. From sharing experiences to supporting new initiatives, there are countless ways to stay connected and contribute to positive change.
Join Us in Our Mission
SLIIT’s community thrives because of the involvement of those who care about its mission. No matter how small or large, your support directly helps students, faculty, and research initiatives grow and evolve.
You can support educational programs and scholarships for deserving students.
You can help improve campus facilities to enhance the learning environment.
You can mentor students, sharing your career expertise and offering guidance.
You can assist in organizing internships, career fairs, and networking events.
Partner with Us to Make a Difference
Collaborate with SLIIT to empower future generations and create lasting impact. Whether you're looking to recruit skilled graduates, engage in innovative research, support student development, or upskill your team, partnering with us opens doors to meaningful opportunities that benefit your organization and the broader community
Recruit Talent
Connect with a pool of highly skilled, job-ready graduates trained in the latest industry practices and ready to make an immediate impact.
Partner in Research
Engage with expert faculty and cutting-edge labs to collaborate on research that drives innovation and delivers practical, real-world solutions.
Invest in Students
Support student scholarships, mentorship, or infrastructure to help shape future leaders and contribute to their academic and personal success.
Train Your Workforce
Upskill your employees through custom training programs, workshops, and certifications designed to meet evolving industry needs and trends.
How can you help us?
Support for SLIIT through time, expertise, resources, or ideas creates meaningful change — enhancing student experiences, advancing research, and impacting communities. Discover how your involvement makes a difference.
Financial Aid and Student Support (Campus Care) Social Impact and Engagement Research and Innovation
Financial Aid and Student Support (Campus Care)
SLIIT is committed to supporting students in need, whether it’s through financial assistance, academic help, or responding to urgent situations.
The Campus Care Fund is designed to respond to these diverse needs, from easing financial pressures to helping students succeed academically. Your involvement can help students stay focused on their education and reach their full potential.
Social Impact and Engagement
We are actively involved in projects that benefit communities, clean-up initiatives, disaster relief, educational support, and more.
Support can take many forms: time, goods, expertise, or funding. To learn more about ongoing projects and how to contribute in a way that suits you, reach out to our outreach team.
Research and Innovation
SLIIT drives impactful research across various disciplines. Whether you're interested in supporting a research initiative, collaborating on an idea, or enabling innovation, there are many ways to get involved.
For more information, you can connect with our research team to explore available opportunities.
