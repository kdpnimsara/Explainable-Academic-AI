Faculty of Engineering Student Community (FESC)
The Faculty of Engineering Student Community at SLIIT was established to represent and support the interests of engineering undergraduates. It brings together students from all disciplines within the faculty to collaborate, engage in academic and extracurricular activities, and contribute to a vibrant university experience.
How to apply?
A course web notice will be published annually, inviting Engineering students to apply for the Faculty of Engineering student community.
