Yapa Appuhamilage Galina Carmel Nadin Basnayake
Faculty of Humanities & Sciences
School of Psychology
Bachelor of Science
From my very first international experience at the 2021 Asian Youth Para Games in Bahrain where I was honored to win three gold medals and one silver, my journey in para-swimming has been both challenging and rewarding.
I remain humbled by the support and encouragement from SLIIT, which drives me to push my limits. Earning a silver medal at the 2023 Hangzhou Asian Para Games further reinforced my belief in myself and my hard work. I am deeply thankful for SLIIT for every opportunity to learn and improve, and I continue to strive for excellence in what I do.
Biography
Yapa Appuhamilage Galina Carmel Nadin Basnayake, a 3rd-year, 2nd-semester student at the Faculty of Humanities and Sciences, Department of Psychology at SLIIT Malabe, is a dedicated para swimmer who has made remarkable strides in both national and international competitions. Her journey in swimming began at a very young age, leading to her debut at the 2021 Asian Youth Para Games in Bahrain, where she secured three gold medals and one silver. This experience laid a strong foundation, fostering gratitude, determination, and a relentless pursuit of excellence. Her success continued at the 2023 Asian Para Games in Hangzhou, China, where she earned silver medal, further fueling her ambition.
In 2024, Galina pursued her goals at both international and national levels. At the World Para Swimming Series in Singapore, she placed 4th in the 400m Freestyle and 10th in the 100m Freestyle. Additionally, she secured a bronze medal at the World Para Swimming Open Water Cup in Sardinia, Italy, competing against elite athletes from the USA and France. Domestically, she showcased her talent at the Long Course and Short Course Nationals, competing in a range of freestyle events from 100m to 1500m, each race reinforcing her resilience and sportsmanship.

International:
1. Citi World Para Swimming Series, Singapore
a. 400m Freestyle: 4th place
b. 100m Freestyle: 10th place

a.3rd place, bronze medal

a.1500m Freestyle: 7th place
b.100, 200, 400, 800m Freestyle: 29th, 17th, 9th, and 9th places respectively
