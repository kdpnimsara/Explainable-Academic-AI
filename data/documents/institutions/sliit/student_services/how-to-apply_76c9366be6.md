How to Apply in Six Steps
Explore the available degree programs and choose the one that matches your academic interests and future goals.
Make sure you meet the academic qualifications and English language requirements for your chosen course.
Fill out and submit the online application form through the official SLIIT admissions portal.
Wait for the admissions team to review your application and contact you with the next steps.
If successful, you will receive your official offer letter via email from SLIIT International.
Accept the offer, meet any remaining conditions, and complete your semester payment to confirm your place.
Student Visa Process
1
Initial Entry Visa
Enter Sri Lanka with a temporary 30-day Entry Visa issued before arrival.
Learn More
Initial Entry Visa
International students must first travel to Sri Lanka on a 30-day Entry Visa. This visa is issued for temporary entry into the country and is a mandatory first step before transitioning to a longer-term Residence Visa. SLIIT provides complete guidance to ensure your initial arrival is smooth and compliant with immigration procedures.
2
Entry Visa Documentation
SLIIT applies on your behalf after receiving required documents.
Learn More
Entry Visa Documentation
SLIIT will apply for your Entry Visa on your behalf before your arrival, once the following documents are submitted:
Translated Birth Certificate
Valid Passport information page
Police Clearance Report from your home country
Health Assessment Report from your home country
Timely submission of these documents is crucial to avoid delays in processing your Entry Visa.
3
Visa Endorsement at the Sri Lankan Mission
Get your visa stamped at the nearest Sri Lankan embassy or high commission.
Learn More
Visa Endorsement at the Sri Lankan Mission
Once your Entry Visa is approved, you must visit the nearest Sri Lankan Embassy or High Commission in your home country. There, the visa will be endorsed (stamped) in your passport. This step confirms your right to enter Sri Lanka under the approved student visa category.
4
Arrival and Residence Visa Application
Convert Entry Visa to a renewable One-Year Residence Visa after arrival.
Learn More
Arrival and Residence Visa Application
After arriving in Sri Lanka, SLIIT will assist you in converting your Entry Visa into a One-Year Residence Visa. This visa must be renewed annually for the duration of your study program. The International Office will guide you through this process to ensure all requirements are met.
5
Residence Visa Requirements
Submit required documents and pay fees for your student Residence Visa.
Learn More
Residence Visa Requirements
To complete the Residence Visa application, you must submit:
Defence and Security Clearance documents
Health Assessment Report (via: ihap.health.gov.lk)
One-Year Residence Visa Fee: USD 200 (payable at www.immigration.gov.lk)
Foreign Exchange Receipt showing USD 1,500 converted
Visa renewals are required yearly and are fully supported by the SLIIT International Office.
