Welcome to SLIIT UNI
Welcome to SLIIT, where your academic journey begins with endless opportunities for growth and discovery. As a leading higher education institute, we are committed to providing a supportive, innovative, and enriching environment for all students.
From worldclass facilities and expert faculty to vibrant campus life and industry connections, SLIIT is the perfect place to shape your future. We’re excited to have you on board and look forward to helping you achieve your dreams.

The payment proof needs to be added in the application portal - apply.sliit.lk
Bank of Ceylon - A/C: 0001630552- Kollupitiya, Super Grade Branch (BOC e-banking interface for SLIIT is also available)
Sampath Bank - A/C: 003990000033 - Malabe Branch (Sampath Vishwa e-banking interface for SLIIT is also available)

Additional information you need to know
When do classes start after enrollment is completed?
Classes typically begin a few weeks after the enrollment deadline. The academic calendar and orientation schedule will be shared with all registered students in advance via email and the SLIIT website.
What documents are required for the enrollment process at SLIIT?
Answer: Students must submit certified copies of their O/L and A/L certificates (or equivalent qualifications), national identity card or passport, birth certificate, and passport-sized photographs.
Is there an application or registration fee when applying to SLIIT?
Answer: Yes, an application processing fee is applicable during the initial application. Upon acceptance, students are also required to pay a registration fee to confirm their enrollment in the selected programme.
How will I know if I have been selected for the programme?
Answer: Once the application is reviewed and eligibility is confirmed, students will receive a Conditional Offer letter or email from SLIIT. This will include instructions on how to proceed with registration and payment.
How can I track my application status, and when will I receive my offer letter?
After you submit your application, you will be notified about the aptitude test schedule via email or SMS. Once you complete the aptitude test and meet the eligibility criteria, your offer letter will be sent to you via email. You can also contact the Enrollment Division or Call Center to check the status of your application at any time.
