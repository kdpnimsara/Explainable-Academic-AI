Faculty of Computing student community
Faculty of Computing Student Community was established under the Faculty of Computing of SLIIT and consists of Computing Undergraduates. Aims at enhancing the knowledge of IT students and accustom them to the ever-changing IT industry. This is done through activities such as workshops and guest lectures by leading IT Professionals in the industry, enabling better student interaction and knowledge-sharing.
How to get help?
Orientation programs at SLIIT University are meticulously designed to offer comprehensive information, valuable resources, and expert guidance to ensure that new students not only navigate but excel in their academic journey. These programs aim to equip students with the necessary tools and knowledge to kickstart their university experience on the right foot, setting them up for success in their educational endeavors.
How to apply
A course web notice will be published annually, inviting Computing students to apply for the Faculty of Student Community.
