Plan Your Stay in Sri Lanka
Accept your offer and apply for your visa early with help from the SLIIT International Office.
Book your on-campus accommodation in advance, as availability is limited and demand is high.
Schedule your flight to Colombo and notify the International Office for optional airport pickup support.
Pack only essentials like documents, light clothing, and electronics, most items are available in Sri Lanka.
You'll receive orientation details and transport assistance after you arrive at Bandaranaike International Airport.
Attend orientation to learn about campus life, academics, student services, and Sri Lankan culture.
Complete your online health assessment and make sure you have valid health or travel insurance.
Discover Sri Lanka’s friendly people, rich culture, tropical beauty, and affordable cost of living.
Reach out to our International Office for help with academics, visas, wellness, and adjusting to life here.
SLIIT ensures a safe, enriching, and exciting experience for all international students from day one.
