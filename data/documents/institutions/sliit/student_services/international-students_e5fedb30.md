FAQs
Will I be guided on finding accommodation upon arrival at SLIIT?
Yes. You will be provided with accommodation options to suit your budget and lifestyle. You can make the final selection based on your preference. You are also free to find your own accommodation independently.
When do I need to arrive at SLIIT?
You should arrive in Sri Lanka at least two weeks prior to the commencement of the Orientation Program to allow time for settling in and completing required formalities.
Do I have to attend the Orientation Program?
Yes. Attendance at the Orientation Program is compulsory. It helps you get familiar with academic processes, support services, and student life at SLIIT.
When do I pay the semester fee?
The semester fee must be paid in full either before your arrival in Sri Lanka or within one week after your arrival, depending on your selected payment plan.
How do I obtain a visa to study in Sri Lanka?
You will first enter Sri Lanka on an entry visa. Immigration officials will endorse a 30 day period on your passport. During this time, your entry visa will be converted into a Resident Visa to continue your studies at SLIIT.
How do I apply for a Resident Visa?
The SLIIT International Office will guide and facilitate your application for a Resident Visa. This process is handled by designated officials to ensure accuracy and compliance.
Will my Resident Visa need to be renewed annually?
Yes. Your Resident Visa must be renewed every year. Renewal is subject to maintaining satisfactory academic performance at SLIIT.
Am I allowed to work part-time while studying in Sri Lanka?
No. According to the Department of Immigration and Emigration in Sri Lanka, international students are not permitted to work while pursuing their studies.
