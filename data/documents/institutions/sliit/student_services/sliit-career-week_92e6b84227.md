SLIIT Career Week
SLIIT Career Week is a dynamic platform where students meet top industry partners under one roof. It offers undergraduates and graduates access to internships, job opportunities, and career insights bridging the gap between academic life and professional success through meaningful industry connections.
Organized by the SLIIT Career Guidance and Counseling Unit, Career Week brings together students and leading industry professionals. It provides a gateway to internships, employment opportunities, and real-world insights. With representatives from IT, Business, and Engineering sectors, the event prepares students for career success and builds lasting professional networks.

Who can participate in Career Week?
All SLIIT undergraduates are encouraged to participate, especially those looking for internships, full-time jobs, or industry exposure.
How can I register for Career Week activities?
Students can register through the official SLIIT Career Guidance Unit (CGU) communication channels or via dedicated event portals shared prior to the event.
What companies take part in the event?
Top companies from sectors like Information Technology, Engineering, and Business participate, offering internships, job placements, and industry sessions.
Is it mandatory to attend Career Week?
Attending is highly recommended as it helps students’ network with employers, improve job-readiness, and explore real-world career pathways.
