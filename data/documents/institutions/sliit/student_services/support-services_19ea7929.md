Student Support Hub
At SLIIT, we are committed to providing comprehensive support services to enhance your academic and personal journey. SLIIT offers full-spectrum student services designed to guide and support your academic and personal development journey.
Essential Student Services
Where can I get study support?
A student can reach out to their respective lecturers, department heads, and the assigned Help Desks for formal study support. Peer-to-peer support is also available.
How can I know my results?
Results can be viewed by the student via their online student portal https://student360.sliit.lk/ They can download a results sheet on their own.They can request a certified results sheet or a level completion transcript via the student support desk https://support.sliit.lk/
How can I get support via staff member?
Student Services divisions are physically located at each campus and centers and can be reached via email, telephone, support desk ticket, as well as by walking into the campus/center premises for any support required. Based on the students’ requirements, the student can be redirected for specific support.
How can I get an official document from the university?
Official documents such as university-issued letters, results sheets, transcripts and certificates etc. can be obtained by making a request via the student support desk that is accessible online https://support.sliit.lk/. Graduates can make a request to the studentservices@sliit.lk https://support.sliit.lk/
Are there scholarship schemes?
Yes, there are performance-based and board-based scholarships available for both new entrants and enrolled students. For new entrants, candidates who have Outstanding Performance in Advanced Level Examination and Outstanding Performance in Sports and curricular Activities in school will be evaluated and granted scholarships for eligible students. For Continuing students, freshmen , and undergraduates who demonstrate outstanding performance in a semester, in sports or extra-curricular activities will be eligible for scholarships. Link https://www.sliit.lk/scholarships/
Easy payment and loans schemes
SLIIT has many partnerships with local banks that offer easy payment and loan schemes. https://www.sliit.lk/bank-loans/ Enrolling students can request a fee structure from the Enrollments Office, and continuing students can request for a studentship confirmation and a fee structure confirmation letter from the student services division and walk-in to any bank branch to check out their available easy payment and loan schemes.
What support is there if I get sick?
SLIIT has dedicated Medical Centers at their respective campuses and center, and the information is available in https://www.sliit.lk/medical-care-within-sliit/
What support is there if I feel distressed or anxious?
SLIIT has a dedicated Unit with Psychologists who will be able to support and the information is available in https://www.sliit.lk/student-life/career-guidance-and counseling/counseling-services/
What kind of sports activities are available for me?
SLIIT offers 22+ indoor and outdoor sports for both men and women, with dedicated premises being available. SLIIT encourages students to participate in sports both recreationally as well as competitively. For more information visit https://www.sliit.lk/student-life/sports/
What kind of extra and co-curricular activities are available for me?
SLIIT has over 50 clubs and societies that provide opportunities for students to exhibit/showcase their talents and improve on their soft and hard skills/leadership skills. These also provide great networking opportunities, such as international exposure. For more information visit https://www.sliit.lk/student-life/clubs-societies/
Why should I do sports or other extra/co-curricular activities at SLIIT?
Engaging in sports and extracurricular activities offers a multitude of benefits that can enhance various aspects of your life. Here are some compelling reasons to consider:
✓ Improved Fitness: Regular physical activity helps to improve cardiovascular health, strength, flexibility, and overall fitness levels.
✓ Weight Management: Engaging in sports can help maintain a healthy weight and reduce the risk of obesity.
✓ Stress Relief: Physical activity releases endorphins, which can reduce stress and improve mood.
✓ Enhanced Focus: Participation in sports can improve concentration and cognitive function, benefiting academic performance.
✓ Teamwork and Cooperation: Sports often require collaboration, helping to develop teamwork and communication skills.
✓ Networking Opportunities: Extracurricular activities provide opportunities to meet new people and build relationships, both with peers and mentors.
✓ Leadership Skills: Many extracurricular activities offer chances to take on leadership roles, enhancing your leadership and organizational abilities.
✓ Discipline and Commitment: Regular participation in an activity requires dedication and discipline, traits that are beneficial in all areas of life.
✓ Balanced Lifestyle: Engaging in activities outside of academics promotes a well rounded and fulfilling lifestyle.
✓ Fun and Enjoyment: Sports and activities provide a break from routine and can be a source of joy and relaxation.
What career development support is there for me?
Other than the subject knowledge that is provided, SLIIT provides an array of opportunities for self and career development. These include Career Counseling, Special Workshops and Seminars led by Industry Experts, Internship and Job Placement opportunities via career fairs, and alumni networking opportunities.
What opportunities are there for professional development?
While SLIIT has its own Professional Development Programs https://www.sliit.lk/professional-programmes/ , SLIIT also links with industries and professional bodies to provide exemptions/reduced fees/scholarships to follow industry recognized professional development programs. SLIIT also has a center for distance learning https://code.sliit.org/
Is SLIIT available only in Colombo?
SLIIT has established many regional campuses and centers offering most of its study programs, providing the opportunity for a student to study while being in the comfort of their home.
How to find a transport service?
Certain campuses and centers provide student transport services from selected locations at a nominal fee https://www.sliit.lk/travel-transport/
