Students Stories
Amarasinghe Arachchige Rashmi Koshila Perera
SLIIT Business School
Bachelor of Science
My passion for athletics began in grade 3, shaping my character and competitive spirit. I proudly represented Sri Lanka at the 3rd Junior South Asian Athletic Championship and consistently secured 1st place in All Island School Meets. At SLIIT, I continued excelling, earning national rankings and selection to the national pool. My journey includes multiple championship victories, SLIIT Colours, and the title of Most Outstanding Sportswoman. Despite injuries, my resilience has driven me to push past challenges. Athletics has been my greatest teacher, instilling perseverance and excellence, and I remain committed to representing my university and country with pride.
Biography
SLIIT proudly recognizes the outstanding achievements of Koshila Amarasinghe an exceptional athlete who has consistently demonstrated excellence in track and field. Upon joining SLIIT, Koshila remained committed to athletics, continuing to compete at the national level and achieving notable rankings. In 2022, she secured second place at the National Selection Trials, third place at the Centenary National Championship, and emerged victorious at the Junior National Championship and NSBM Usports. These accomplishments led to the prestigious recognition of SLIIT Colours for track and field, the title of Best Player in Track and Field, and the honor of being named the Most Outstanding Sportswoman of the Year. The momentum continued in 2023, where Koshila claimed third place at both the National Selection Trials and the 101st National Championship, once again earning SLIIT Colours for track and field, as well as for basketball.
The following year, 2024, marked another remarkable chapter in her journey, with first place finishes at the National Selection Trials and the 48th National Sports Games, culminating in a selection to the South Asia Senior Championship National Pool. In 2025, Koshila continued to prove her dominance in the sport by securing first place at the National First Selection Trials. SLIIT is proud to support and celebrate Koshila Amarasinhe’s inspiring journey, recognizing the dedication and passion that continue to elevate her success in athletics and beyond.
