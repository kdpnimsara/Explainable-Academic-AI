The Faculty of Humanities & Sciences Student Community
The Faculty of Humanities & Sciences Student Community at SLIIT was formed to represent and support undergraduates within the faculty. It provides a platform for students to engage in academic, leadership, and extracurricular activities, fostering collaboration, personal growth, and a vibrant learning environment across diverse disciplines in the humanities and sciences.
How to apply?
A course web notice will be published annually, inviting Humanities & Sciences students to apply for the Faculty of Humanities & Sciences student community.
