Medical Care within SLIIT
Ensuring our community's well-being, SLIIT proudly offers on-campus healthcare services, including a full-time in-house doctor available for consultations. Additionally, our premises have a Suwasariya ambulance service, providing rapid medical assistance and emergency response. These facilities underscore our dedication to creating a safe and supportive environment for our students, staff, and visitors, fostering a holistic approach to education and campus life.

Travel & Transport
In Sri Lanka, public buses have traditionally been the primary mode of transportation for most people. However, the emergence of private transportation services like Uber and PickMe has significantly enhanced convenience and accessibility for commuters. SLIIT, recognizing the importance of transportation for its students, offers shuttle services along various nearby routes, further facilitating ease of travel to and from the campus. Furthermore, an added advantage is the Kothalawala entrance to the Southern Expressway being just 1.5km away from SLIIT premises.
English Language Help Desk
SLIIT's English Support Help Desk caters to both International and Local Students, providing invaluable assistance in enhancing their English proficiency. This dedicated service offers a supportive environment where students can refine their language skills through tailored guidance, resources, and practice opportunities. By fostering a collaborative and inclusive learning atmosphere, SLIIT empowers students to communicate effectively, build confidence, and excel academically and professionally in an English-speaking context.
Places of Worship
Malabe, a suburb of Colombo, is home to several religious sites of different faiths. The area has a predominantly Sinhalese Buddhist population, with the Mahamevnawa Buddhist Monastery being a prominent place of worship. These religious places offer visitors a glimpse into the diverse religious landscape of Malabe and its surrounding communities.

SLIIT Buddies
SLIIT's International Student Buddy Program enables new international students to transition into the university environment. Each student is paired with a current student, providing cultural, and practical support about life at SLIIT and in Sri Lanka. This one-month program fosters a diverse, inclusive, and supportive community for international students.
