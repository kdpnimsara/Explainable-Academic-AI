Career Guidance
SLIIT Career Guidance Unit
The SLIIT Career Guidance (CGU) Unit equips students with essential skills and connects them to top industry partners. It enhances students’ understanding of career paths, technical and soft skills, and job opportunities. Through targeted events, workshops, and services, CGU helps students balance academics and career goals.
How we support you
Our Collaborations
SLIIT’s study programs are developed with industry partners, enabling professionals to engage in student activities by sharing expertise, resources, and real-world insights that strengthen academic and career readiness.
View More
Jobs and Internships
SLIIT collaborates with leading industries to offer structured internship programs that match students' academic focus, providing valuable hands-on experience and exposure to real-world professional environments.
View More
Career Coaching
SLIIT Career Coaching provides personalized guidance, career counseling, and access to industry insights, helping students build experience, enhance confidence, and successfully transition into the professional job market.
View More
Guidance Workshops
Workshops focus on soft skills like communication, teamwork, and leadership. Industry experts lead seminars and guest lectures to share market insights and in-demand skills with students.

HCL Career day organized by SLIIT CGU
HCL Career day organized by SLIIT Career Guidance Unit was successfully held on the 16th and 17th December 2021 with 200+ SLIIT graduates and undergraduates participating for the event on campus.
The CVs were collected from the participants and they were able to get an idea about the multiple vacancies that are available in HCL Technologies Lanka along with valuable insights on how to become a well-known IT professional. A one-to-one discussion was arranged for the graduates and undergraduates to develop their career.
