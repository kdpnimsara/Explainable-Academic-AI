# SLIIT Notice: Reference Sheets to Be Used During Examinations

**Source date:** May 2025
**Source authority:** Director, Academic Affairs and Student Services, SLIIT
**Source URL:** https://courseweb.sliit.lk/pluginfile.php/113/mod_forum/attachment/10191/Introduction%20of%20Reference%20Sheets%20to%20be%20used%20during%20Examinations.pdf?forcedownload=1

## When a reference sheet may be used

The notice says that, following a Senate recommendation, faculties may permit one self-created A4-size reference sheet in final examinations **at the discretion of the Lecturer-in-Charge (LIC)**. It is therefore not a universal entitlement for every module or examination. Students should follow the instructions given by the LIC for the relevant module.

## Requirements stated in the notice

- The reference sheet is limited to one A4 page, handwritten or printed, and may use one or both sides.
- It should be concise and contain only material taught during the module.
- Sample answers must not be included.
- Students must not share reference sheets during examinations; each student prepares and uses their own sheet.
- The module name and examination candidate's index number must appear at the top.
- The reference sheet must be attached to the answer script.
- The LIC may provide further module-specific instructions.

## Academic misconduct warning

The notice refers to an update to Section 2.8 of the SLIIT Student Rule Book. Possession of unauthorised notes or materials and copying/attempting to copy are identified as examination misconduct. The notice says consequences can include failing the examination, suspension, or expulsion.

## Important limitations

This summary represents the May 2025 notice only. It must not be treated as confirmation that reference sheets are permitted in every current examination. Students should check the latest CourseWeb notice and obtain module-specific confirmation from the LIC.
