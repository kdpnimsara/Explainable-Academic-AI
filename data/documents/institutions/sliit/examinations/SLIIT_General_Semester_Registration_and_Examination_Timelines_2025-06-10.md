# SLIIT General Timelines for Semester and Module Registration and Examinations

**Source publication date:** 10 June 2025
**Source authority:** SLIIT Academic Affairs / SLIIT Support Desk
**Source URL:** https://support.sliit.lk/kb/articles/pdf/general-timelines-for-semester-module-registration-and-examination

## Important status and limitations

This is a general guideline published by SLIIT on 10 June 2025. The source explicitly says the timelines are subject to change based on institutional requirements and other specific situations. It also says CourseWeb is the primary communication platform for official student information and that registration and examination timelines are announced through CourseWeb notices. This document is not a current individual student's timetable or a guarantee of dates for a particular intake.

## General semester timelines stated by SLIIT

| Activity | General timing | January–June semester | July–December semester |
|---|---|---|---|
| Semester registration | Four weeks before semester commencement | December / January | May / June |
| Late semester registration (penalty fees apply) | One week into semester commencement | January | June |
| Semester commencement | — | February | July |
| Elective registration | Two weeks after semester commencement | February | July |
| Module registration | Three weeks after semester commencement at the latest | March | August |
| Mid examination | Eight weeks after semester commencement | April | September |
| Final examination | Fifteen weeks after semester commencement | May / June | October / November |
| Provisional results release | — | July | December |

## Guidance for answering student questions

- Treat the table as a general planning guide only, not as a confirmed date list for a current semester.
- For exact registration deadlines, examination dates, timetable changes, or result-release dates, direct students to the latest official CourseWeb notices and their relevant faculty or Academic Affairs office.
- Do not infer a specific date from the week number or month shown in this general guide.
- Where a programme is awarded by a partner university, check whether the partner university's rules and schedule apply.
