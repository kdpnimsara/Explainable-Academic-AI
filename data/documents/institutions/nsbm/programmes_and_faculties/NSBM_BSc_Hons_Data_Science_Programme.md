BSC (HONOURS) IN DATA SCIENCE
Approved by Ministry of Higher Education – OFFERED BY NSBM
Duration -
4 Years (Full time)
Enroll Now
BSc (Honors) in Data Science programme at NSBM Faculty of Computing is carefully designed in line with the latest ACM and IEEE guidelines for Data Science undergraduate awards and aims to produce graduates with the required skills and knowledge in core and emerging trends in the technological and business disciplines.
During the first year, students will develop their foundational knowledge in computing, which is mandatory to absorb Data Science related concepts and their applications. From second year onwards, gradually the Data Science flavor of the degree programme is escalating. In the third year, whilst sharpening their skills on data science, they ought to fulfill a mandatory industry placement. In the fourth year, they are being guided to pursue an individual research project to showcase the expertise they have developed through the Programme.
KEY FEATURES
UGC Approved 4-year honor’s degree with a full emphasize towards Data Science.
A diverse choice of elective modules to choose from, in their final year of studies, to cater to individual preferences and specializations.
Includes mandatory industry placement and supervised research projects element.
Creating the foundation for a rewarding lucrative carrier in Data Science.
Aims of the Program
Develop proficiency in analysis of complex problems and synthesize software solutions with the latest concept for an organization/ individual.
Develop lone learning aptitude to acquire new knowledge required for an assignment which associated with novel concepts.
Admire intellectual works of others and to abide by industry norms and ethics stipulated by professional bodies.
Satisfy the academic criteria required for the membership of professional institutions.
