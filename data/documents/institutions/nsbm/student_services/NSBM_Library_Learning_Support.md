📚 Library Helpline Service
Need help with library services? The NSBM Library Helpline is here to support you.
You can use this service to:
Request a Book or Article
Can't find a specific book or article in our collection? Submit a request, and we'll help you access the resources you need for your studies or research.
Click Here to Request
Ask About Other Library-related Queries
Have questions about library services, operating hours, membership, borrowing policies, or anything else? Our team is ready to assist you with any library-related inquiries.
Click Here to Ask
How It Works
Submit your request through the link provided above, and our library team will respond as soon as possible. For urgent matters, please contact us directly via phone or email.
📖 Suggest a New Book or Resource
NSBM Library Services welcomes your suggestions for new books, journals, or resources to support your learning and research.
About This Service
At NSBM Library Services, we aim to provide books and other resources that support your learning and research. If there is a book, journal, or other material that we don't currently have, we welcome your suggestions for purchase.
Making a Request
Requests can be made by NSBM Library members.
Before submitting a request, please check the library catalogue to ensure we don't already have the item.
To process your request quickly, please provide as much detail as possible.
Use a separate form for each item you suggest.
All requests will be considered by the Library, and we will inform you whether the item is being purchased.
Need it urgently?
If required sooner, we may be able to obtain the item from a nearby library or via an Inter-Library Loan. Please contact our Library Help Team for further assistance.
